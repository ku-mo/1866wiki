# New Alliance Guide


:::info Guide
Procedural reference for alliances interacting with NAP 9. This guide explains how to enter the system, what protections apply, and what obligations follow.
:::


NAP 9 recognizes three states: **Full Member**, **Associate Member**, and **Non-Member**. Each carries different rights and responsibilities.

---

# 1. Choosing Your Status

## 1.1 Associate Member (starting path)
- Non-voting participant.  
- Eligible for NAP protection on **L1–L5 land currently held**.  
- Can submit evidence and request case review.  
- Must follow all penalties, compliance rules, and Clerk procedures.  

This is the standard entry point for alliances seeking stability.

## 1.2 Full Member (governance seat)
- Occupies one of the **nine Council seats**.  
- Gains voting authority on all cases and amendments.  
- Receives full land protection for territories held.  
- Must maintain high participation and strict procedural compliance.  

Full Membership is not requested, it is earned through ranking and standing.

## 1.3 Non-Member (independent)
- No NAP protection.  
- No case rights, no appeals, no land coverage.  
- Interactions occur entirely outside the NAP system.

---

# 2. Joining as an Associate Member

Associate capacity is **seven (7)** seats, first-come, first-served.

## 2.1 Required Information
Your R5 must post the following in  
`NAP Discord → #associate-applications`:

- **Alliance Name + Tag**  
- **R5 In-Game Name**  
- **R5 Discord Tag** (If applicant is not R5) 
- Statement of acceptance of the NAP 9 Covenant:  
  > “I accept all standards and procedures of the NAP 9 Covenant.”  
- Statement of evidence/penalty compliance:  
  > “I will honor all evidence rules and enforcement requirements.”

## 2.2 Clerk Verification
Clerks confirm:
- no active penalties or investigations  
- no harboring of blacklisted players  
- an Associate slot is available  

If compliant → admission is **immediate**.  
If full → the alliance is placed in the **timestamped waiting queue**.

## 2.3 Opt-Out Procedure
If leaving by choice:
1. R5 posts in `#associate-announcements`:  
   > “We are ending our alliance's participation NAP 9 effectively immediately.”  
2. All NAP protection ends immediately.  
3. A **7-day cooldown** begins before re-application.

---

# 3. Path to Full Membership

Full Membership occurs **automatically** when eligible.

## 3.1 Eligibility
An alliance becomes a Full Member when:
1. It ranks within the **Top 9** by weekly power metrics (skipping ineligible alliances).  
2. It has affirmed the Covenant.  
3. It meets representation requirements.

## 3.2 Representation Requirements
A Full Member must appoint:
- **1 Voting Representative (primary)**  
- **1 Alternate (delegate)**

Both must be active in NAP Discord governance channels.

## 3.3 Automatic Promotion / Demotion
When rankings update:
- The highest-ranked **eligible Associate** is promoted into the vacated seat.  
- The displaced Full Member becomes an Associate.  
- No vote or request is required.

If no Associate exists, the displaced alliance becomes a **Priority Associate** temporarily.

---

# 4. Protections and Limitations

## 4.1 Full Members
Protected for all lands currently held.  
Eligible to vote, propose amendments, and access all governance records.

## 4.2 Associates
Protected for **L1–L5** lands currently held.  
May file evidence and request rulings but **do not vote**.

## 4.3 Non-Members
Protected for nothing.  
All land and disputes occur outside NAP jurisdiction.

---

# 5. Obligations Inside NAP

All alliances inside NAP—Full or Associate—must:

- Submit evidence only in `#evidence` with Server Time and required items.  
- Wait for Clerk Case ID before engaging in case activity.  
- Enforce penalties internally (bans, restitutions, tag removals).  
- Avoid sheltering blacklisted players or evasion attempts.  
- Respond to Clerks when procedural input is required.  
- Follow Server Time for all deadlines and vote windows.  

Silence, non-response, or refusal to enforce rulings can result in removal.

---

# 6. Operational Contacts

- **Clerks** → procedural questions (evidence, timestamps, IDs, deadlines).  
- **Council Representatives** → land disputes, diplomacy, case context.  
- **NAP Channels**:  
  - `#case-log` — active cases  
  - `#nap-announcements` — binding decisions  
  - `#nap-council-chat` — Council discussion (read-only for Associates)  
  - `#nap-proposals` — amendments and notices  

In-game chat is **not** authoritative for governance.
