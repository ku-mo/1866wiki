# New Alliance Guide
How a new or growing alliance can work with NAP 9 for protection, land stability, and predictable diplomacy.

---

## What NAP 9 Does for You
- Protects your members from random hits if you are inside NAP (Full or Associate).  
- Standardizes evidence, penalties, and appeals so disputes don’t become chaos.  
- Keeps Level 6 land rotation stable and predictable.

---

## Your Options
**Associate Member (recommended starting point)**  
- Join the protection system without voting.  
- Protection for **L1–L5**; cannot hold L6.  
- Access to evidence submission and announcements.

**Non-Member (independent)**  
- No protection, no case rights.  
- Must drop any L6 immediately; L1–L5 are unprotected.

---

## How to Join as an Associate (First-Come, First-Serve, Max 7)
1. R5 posts a petition in `NAP Discord → #associate-applications` with alliance name/tag, R5 + Discord ID, and acceptance of NAP rules.  
2. Clerk verifies you are not blacklisted, not harboring blacklisted players, and a slot is open.  
3. If space is open, admission is **automatic**. If full, you enter the timestamped waiting queue.

Opting out later: declare publicly → lose protections immediately + 7-day cooldown before reapplying.

---

## How to Reach Full Membership
- Climb into the **Top 9 alliances by weekly power ranking** and opt in.  
- You get one Voting Representative + one Alternate, full NAP protection (L1–L6), and rotation eligibility.  
- Expectation: vote reliably, honor land rules, enforce penalties.

---

## Your Obligations Inside NAP
- Follow Server Time deadlines.  
- Submit evidence only in `#evidence`; wait for Clerk Case ID before action.  
- Comply with penalties and bans; do not shelter evaders or blacklisted players.  
- For Associates: never hold L6; drop immediately if asked.  
- For all: do not tag-drop to attack—second time is automatic permanent blacklist.

---

## Quick Contact Paths
- Your R5/Delegate → for diplomacy and votes.  
- Clerks → procedural questions only (evidence, timing, IDs).  
- `#nap-announcements` → official rulings; `#case-log` → active cases.

---

## TL;DR for New Alliances
- Join as Associate to get protection while you grow.  
- If you opt out, you are unprotected and must drop L6.  
- Evidence → `#evidence`; wait for Case ID.  
- Stay responsive; silence has consequences.***
