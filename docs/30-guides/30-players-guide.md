# 🧭 NAP 9 — Player Guide

:::info Guide
A simple, practical guide for everyday players.  
:::

This document explains **how to use NAP 9 to your advantage**, how to protect your alliance, and what tools you actually need.  

No legalese. No walls of text. Just the parts that matter to you.

---

# ⭐ Why NAP 9 Matters to YOU

NAP 9 gives players:

- **Protection** — your lands and trucks are covered when other players break rules  
- **Predictability** — cases follow the same steps every single time  
- **Power** — ANY player can open a case  
- **Receipts** — everything is logged and auditable  
- **Stability** — alliances can’t “vibe-judge” you anymore; the system handles it  

If you follow the steps here, **you can’t be ignored, gaslit, or brushed off**.  
NAP 9 forces fair handling.

---

# 🛡️ When Should *YOU* Use NAP 9?

Use the system whenever another alliance:

- plunders your Truck / Secret Task  
- hits your tile or base without permission  
- refuses compensation  
- repeatedly griefs you
- ignores diplomacy  
- hides banned/blacklisted players  
- evades penalties

If anything feels **unfair, destabilizing, or repetitive**, NAP 9 is made specifically to handle it.

---

# 📸 Step 1 — Capture Evidence (Your Most Important Job)

Take **1–3 screenshots**:

1. **Offender visible** (IGN clearly shown)  
2. **Timestamp** (Server Time screens preferred)  
3. **Context** — what they hit or plundered  

Avoid:

- cropped images  
- DMs  
- AI images  
- collages  
- after-the-fact explanations without proof  

If you can’t show it, it didn’t happen.

---

# 📤 Step 2 — Submit Evidence in '''#evidence'''

Post the following:

- screenshots  
- server time  
- offender name  
- short (≤3 sentences) explanation  
- diplomacy attempts (if any)  

Example macro:

```
**Incident:** Truck Plunder  
**Offender:** NastyToast (Alliance TAG)  
**Victim:** MagicMouse  
**Time (ST):** 14:22  
**Summary:** Player plundered my UR truck during an active run.  
**Evidence:** [screenshots attached]
```

That’s all you need.  
Do **NOT** DM Clerks—public evidence protects you.

---

# 🏛️ Step 3 — Wait for the Clerk Log

A Clerk will:

- check the screenshots  
- request fixes if needed  
- assign a **Case ID**  
- post it in '''#case-log'''  
- mark the case **ACTIVE**

Once ACTIVE → the Council is REQUIRED to handle your case.

No alliance can bury, silence, or derail it.

---

# ⚖️ How Cases Work (Player Version)

NAP 9 categorizes behavior:

| Tier | Meaning | Player Examples |
|------|---------|-----------------|
| 🟨 Minor | accidental / small harm | accidental tile hit, 1-time plunder |
| 🟧 Moderate | repeated / harmful | repeated plunders, refusing diplomacy |
| 🟥 Severe | major harm / evasion | evidence tampering, coordinated attacks, hiding banned players |

Escalation is automatic:  
- 2 Minors → Moderate  
- 2 Moderates → Severe  

Severe happens FAST if someone is repeatedly griefing the server.

---

# 💰 Penalties That Benefit YOU

Penalties after Council vote may include:

- warned player  
- required **restitution** (resources back to you)  
- bans/expulsions
- watchlists  
- blacklists  
- alliance sanctions  

If you’re the victim, YOU get restitution.

# 🧨 What If They Keep Doing It?

Good news: NAP 9 **remembers patterns**, even if YOU don’t know the history.

If someone is a chronic offender:

- Clerks recognize repeat offenders  
- Cases escalate automatically  
- Penalties become harsher  
- Their alliance may be sanctioned  
- They may be banned or blacklisted  

You don’t need to know their past.

**The system will handle it.**

---

# 🛠️ Using Amendments (Players Can Influence Laws)

NAP 9 lets alliances **add new rules, new penalties, or clarify systems** through amendments.

## Players cannot submit amendments directly  
BUT you **can**:

1. ask your Full-Member R5 or Delegate to sponsor one  
2. coordinate with your alliance leadership  
3. work with two other Associate Alliances (if you are one)  
   - three Associate Alliances together can propose an amendment  

Amendments shape the future rules of the server.

---

# 🧰 Amendment Macro (for your R5)

```
**Proposed Amendment:** Add truck/secret task fines  
**Type:** Additive  
**Section:** Title 7 (Offense Categories)  
**Text:**  
"Unauthorized plunder of Trucks or Secret Tasks is a Minor Offense on first occurrence and a Moderate Offense on subsequent occurrences within 30 days. Standard restitution is required: equal to the resource loss documented in evidence."  
**Justification:** Clarifies treatment of truck/ST plundering and improves consistency.  
**Retroactive:** No  
**Activation:** Immediate  
```

You can use this for ANY rule you want to add.

---

# 🪢 Appeals (Players Can Trigger These Too)

If you think a penalty against your alliance was unfair:

1. ask your R5 to file an appeal  
2. Clerks validate  
3. investigation happens  
4. Council votes  

If 5 YES → penalty reduced  
If 7 YES → penalty overturned  

Appeals keep power balanced.

# 🏰 How To Protect Your Alliance

Below is a compact, player-friendly table that turns the five key protection rules into clear, actionable guidance.

| 🔰 Rule | 📌 What You Should Do | 💡 Why It Matters |
|--------|------------------------|-------------------|
| **1. Report everything** | Always submit incidents to **#evidence**, even Minor ones. | Every confirmed incident builds a **record**, which fuels escalation rules and strengthens your alliance's protection. |
| **2. Keep screenshots** | Save your screenshots/videos for **30 days** with timestamps. | The system may not always be perfect. You may be able to prove patterns that weren't otherwise picked up on. |
| **3. Encourage leadership to vote** | Make sure your R5/Delegate participates in all votes. | Silent Council seats weaken quorum, slow rulings, and can trigger **participation penalties** that hurt your alliance. |
| **4. Don’t harbor banned players** | Make sure banned/blacklisted players are kicked within **4 hours** of Clerk notice. | Harboring immediately removes **all NAP protection** from your alliance until compliance is confirmed. |
| **5. Follow diplomacy rules** | Communicate clearly before aggression; keep logs when needed. | Good diplomacy avoids penalties, supports case outcomes, and strengthens your alliance’s reputation and bargaining position. |

# 🧩 Common Player Questions

### **Do I need to talk to the offender first?**
Not required, but helpful.  
If they ignore you, the case *may* become stronger.

### **What if the Clerk asks for more evidence?**
Just post the missing items.  
You get **6 hours** to fix it.

### **Can the offender retaliate?**
If they do, you now have *two* cases.

### **Can normal players check case history?**
Yes, `#case-log` is public to Members.

### **Can I get in trouble by accident?**
If it’s accidental and you cooperate, it’s easily only a Minor.
