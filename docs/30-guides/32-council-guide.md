# NAP 9 Council Guide
Operational manual for R5s and Delegates on the nine-seat Council.

---

## Your Role
- Represent your alliance and vote on every case.  
- Review evidence and classify behavior by rule text.  
- Engage in diplomacy professionally; avoid threats or drama.  
- Ensure your alliance complies with penalties and land rules.

You **do not** handle evidence validation (Clerks do) or judge intent beyond category fit.

---

## Participation Requirements
- Target **≥80%** vote participation.  
- Missing **2 Emergency votes** or **3 votes in 14 days** → Council review (possible rotation suspension, downgrade to Associate, or temporary loss of vote).

---

## Communication Protocols
- Use NAP Discord only: `#nap-council-chat` (deliberation), `#case-log` (active cases), `#nap-announcements` (rulings), `#nap-proposals` (amendments).  
- In-game chat is **not binding**.  
- Do not DM Clerks evidence or pressure them on outcomes.

---

## How to Engage in Cases
When a case is marked **ACTIVE** in `#case-log`:

1. Read the Case ID post and evidence.  
2. Ask Clerks for procedural clarity if needed (timestamps, classification).  
3. Map behavior to Minor/Moderate/Severe definitions.  
4. Participate in discussion briefly, then vote.

**Challenge evidence** by requesting verification—never by accusing or attacking.

---

## Voting Mechanics
- Vote with explicit text: `TAG — YES/NO/ABSTAIN`. Reactions/phrases like “lean yes” do not count.  
- Votes are **locked** once cast.  
- **Quorum:** 5 votes. **Simple Majority:** 5 YES. **Supermajority:** 7 YES (blacklists, expulsions, rotation removal, amendments, overturn Severe).  
- Abstain counts for quorum and is appropriate for conflicts or uncertainty.  
- If all 9 votes arrive early, Clerks may close early.

---

## Conflict of Interest
- Recuse (or ABSTAIN) when your alliance is the offender, victim, or targeted by the amendment/penalty.  
- Alternate votes instead; if both conflicted, the seat auto-ABSTAINS. Do not lobby others on conflicted matters.

---

## Land Governance Duties
- Ensure your alliance honors L6 rotation drops/pickups.  
- Report rotation violations promptly.  
- Non-Members have zero land protection; coordinate seizures responsibly if required.

---

## Emergency Response
- For active attacks/tag-drops/L6 conflicts: alert Clerks, request an **8h Emergency Vote**, supply timestamped evidence, and coordinate ceasefire steps per vote results.

---

## Best Practices
- Vote early; keep notes for your alliance.  
- Stay concise in discussions—focus on rule fit, not intent debates.  
- Use macros for clarity (e.g., “Requesting timestamp verification”).  
- Maintain respectful tone with Clerks and other reps.  
- Avoid backchannel lobbying; keep deliberation visible and procedural.
