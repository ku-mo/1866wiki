# 🏛️ Council Inception Protocol  

:::info Scenario
*How Server 1866 forms the very first NAP 9 Council*
:::

The following document describes **exactly how the initial NAP 9 Council is created**, how temporary authorities function, and how the system transitions into full procedural governance. 

---

# 1. Purpose of the Inception Process

NAP 9 requires:

- **9 Full Member seats**  
- **Clerks from 3 different alliances**  
- **Working Discord infrastructure**  
- **Verified voting representatives**  

But none of this exists on Day 0.  
The **Council Inception Protocol** creates a **controlled bridge** between:

- the legacy NAP (if any)  
- unstructured diplomacy  
- the fully operational NAP 9 system  

This phase MUST be **short**, **neutral**, and **transparent**.

---

# 2. The Transitionary Council

:::caution Important
A temporary body known as **the Transitionary Council** is formed to bootstrap the system.
:::

> **The KY Colonel** and **sheriff girliion** are appointed to assist with information dissemination and diplomacy.

### Requirements
- Minimum **5 alliances**, ideally more  
- Each alliance designates an R5 or diplomat as its provisional rep  
- These reps do *not* hold permanent Council seats  
- They exist only to stand up the structure

### Duties
The Transitionary Council:

- Approves the creation of the NAP 9 Discord  
- Confirms the temporary Clerks  
- Invites eligible alliances  
- Communicates reforms to the server  
- Oversees setup of roles, channels, and permissions  
- Ensures no single alliance exerts disproportionate control during setup

### Duration
- Minimum: **24 hours**  
- Maximum: **72 hours**  

After that, the body dissolves automatically and is replaced by the Council.

---

# 3. Transitionary Clerks (Bootstrap Operators)

:::caution Important
Until permanent Clerks are elected, the system requires neutral technicians.  
:::


These bootstrap operators:

- validate early membership petitions  
- maintain the channel structure  
- support the import of alliances  
- coordinate sign-offs  
- ensure the Covenant is published unedited

### Named Individuals
- **deputy konpy**  
- **potekima**
- **ZoeKween**  

These individuals serve ONLY until the permanent election occurs.

They **cannot** open cases, classify offenses, issue penalties, or initiate any binding votes unless it concerns the *inception process itself.*

---

# 4. Standing Up the Discord Server

:::caution Important
Currently various members of GMOB who have volunteered are anticipated to support the transition.
:::


> **deputy konpy** (as primary server architect) performs:

- channel creation  
- role stubs  
- Clerk permissions  
- Council permissions  
- evidence/log architecture  
- pinning rules  
- read-only / vote windows structure  

> **ZoeKween** assists with creation and auditing server functionality.

> **potekima** assists with supporting NAP knowledge.

This server becomes the **permanent home** for NAP 9 governance.

---

# 5. Onboarding Alliances Into NAP 9

:::important Roles
What must be done for each expected alliance during this phase
:::

### 1. Eligible Full Members
Identify the **top 9 alliances by weekly power ranking**.  
Invite their R5 + Delegate to the server.

### 2. Associates
If alliances request Associate status during the transition period, they may be placed:

- directly into the Associate list (if slots exist), **or**  
- queued in the timestamped waitlist  

### 3. Non-Members
They receive information only.  
They cannot interact with cases or governance.

---

# 6. Activating the First Real Council

:::important Activation
Once the server infrastructure and membership roster are complete, these steps activate the first Council
:::

### Step 1 — Confirm the 9 Full Member seats  
- Each alliance provides:  
  - Voting Rep  
  - Alternate Delegate  
- Transitionary Clerks verify the list  

### Step 2 — Initial Council convenes  
Transitionary Clerks summon the first meeting:

```
@Council — Please acknowledge that you are ready to constitute the First Council of NAP 9. Once all nine seats confirm, the Transitionary Council will dissolve.
```

### Step 3 — Elect permanent Clerks  
NAP 9 requires **three Clerks from three different alliances**.

Process:

```
1. Nominations open for 12 hours  
2. Discussion window (optional)  
3. 16h vote  
4. Top 3 (meeting eligibility rules) become the inaugural Clerk cohort
```

### Step 4 — Dissolution of the Transitionary Council  
This is automatic AND mandatory.

```
The Transitionary Council dissolves.  
Transitionary Clerks revert to normal leadership roles.  
NAP 9 enters full operational state.
```

---

# 7. Post-Inception Stabilization

Once the first Council and Clerk cohort are active:

- the Clerk team confirms evidence standards  
- the Council confirms communication norms  
- the server begins accepting live cases  
- the amendment pipeline opens  
- Associate queue becomes active  
- participation tracking begins  

This moment marks **Day 1** of NAP 9 governance.

---

# 8. TL;DR Version (Player-Friendly)

```
1. Get 5+ alliances to agree NAP 9 should begin  
2. Create a transitionary council (24–72h)  
3. Konpy builds the server; various GMOB members support  
4. Invite all top 9 alliances  
5. Bring in Associates, create waitlist  
6. First real Council confirms itself  
7. Elect 3 Clerks  
8. Transitionary structures dissolve  
9. NAP 9 starts for real
```
