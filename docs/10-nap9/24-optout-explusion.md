# Opt-Outs & Expulsions
How alliances leave or are removed from NAP 9, and what happens next.

---

## Opt-Out (Voluntary)
- Declare publicly in `#nap-announcements`.  
- Immediate consequences: loss of all NAP protection, loss of L1–L5 protection, **mandatory release of all L6** (may be forcibly seized), no evidence/appeal rights.  
- **7-day cooldown** before re-entry.

---

## Expulsion (Supermajority)
- Grounds: harboring blacklisted players, refusing penalties, repeated non-responsiveness, undermining operations, coordinated misconduct, cross-server collusion.  
- Requires **Supermajority (7 YES)**.  
- Consequences mirror opt-out plus **30-day loss of rights** by default.

---

## Reinstatement
- File a petition after cooldown/compliance.  
- Requires **Simple Majority** approval and full compliance with outstanding rulings.

---

## Associate Removal
- Associates may be removed by **Simple Majority** for non-response, interfering with governance, refusing rulings, or harboring blacklisted players. Vacancies fill from the waiting queue.

---

## Practical Notes
- NAP protections end **immediately** on opt-out/expulsion—do not rely on grace periods.  
- L6 seizures from departing alliances may be handled via Emergency vote if contested.***
