# Preamble

We, the alliances of Server 1866, establish this Covenant to provide predictable governance, protect member-held lands, and resolve disputes through transparent process rather than ad hoc force. We commit to:

- Maintain a nine-seat Council with neutral Clerk oversight.  
- Uphold strict evidence standards and recorded votes.  
- Apply penalties and protections consistently across members.  
- Adapt by amendment, not by exception.  
- Preserve Server Time as the single clock for all actions.

This Preamble states purpose only. Binding rules, rights, and obligations begin in Title 2 and subsequent Titles.
