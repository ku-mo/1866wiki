# Offense Categories
NAP 9 classifies behavior into **Minor**, **Moderate**, and **Severe** tiers to keep penalties predictable. Escalation rules apply across tiers.

---

## 🟨 Minor Offenses
Low-impact, accidental, or isolated violations with minimal loss.

**Examples**
- Accidental tile/base contact  
- Mild chat or diplomacy delay  
- Minor disruption to rotation handoff (non-malicious)

**Required Evidence**
- Timestamped screenshot/video  
- ≤3 sentence description  
- Damage summary (if any)  
- Diplomacy context when relevant

**Standard Penalties**
- Logged warning + restitution  
- **24–48h Probation** (no non-capitol PvP; violation auto-escalates to Moderate)

Mitigating: apology, self-reporting, clean 30-day history.  
Aggravating: repeated minor behavior, non-response, casual negligence.

---

## 🟧 Moderate Offenses
Meaningful disruption, repeated violations, or harmful actions without clear malice.

**Examples**
- Repeated task/truck plundering  
- Aggressive tile hits without tag drop  
- Pattern of diplomacy refusal  
- Obstructing L6 temporary drops  
- Violating Minor probation

**Required Evidence**
- Timestamps and pattern demonstration  
- Diplomacy attempts (required)  
- Damage/loss summary  
- Clerk case validation

**Standard Penalties**
- Mandatory restitution  
- **48–72h Combat Restriction** (no offensive PvP outside Capitol)  
- Temporary rotation suspension (player or alliance)  
- **Moderate Watchlist** for 14 days

Mitigating: rapid restitution, full cooperation.  
Aggravating: repeat within 30 days, multi-player involvement, defiance of instructions.

---

## 🟥 Severe Offenses
High intent, high impact, coordinated aggression, destabilization, or evasion.

**Examples**
- Coordinated attacks on NAP alliances  
- L6 land theft or obstruction  
- Cross-server collusion against 1866  
- Targeted harassment  
- Penalty evasion (ban dodging, tag hopping)  
- Evidence tampering  
- Harboring a banned/blacklisted player **after official notice** (not removed within 4 hours)  
- Repeated Moderate offenses

**Required Evidence**
- Verified timestamps (multi-source when possible)  
- Diplomacy logs  
- Impact statement  
- Clerk validation

**Penalties (Simple Majority: ≥5 YES)**
- **72h serverwide ban** (untagged)  
- **7-day ban** from all alliances  
- Restitution and Severe review record

**Penalties (Supermajority: ≥7 YES)**
- **Permanent blacklist**  
- **Alliance sanctions:** rotation removal, temporary expulsion/demotion to Associate, suspension of protections  
- **Permanent alliance expulsion** or KOS designation

---

## Notes on Penalties
- Council may adjust within the tier if evidence shows strong mitigation or aggravation.  
- Appeals are only available for penalties **longer than 48 hours**; see Appeals section.***
