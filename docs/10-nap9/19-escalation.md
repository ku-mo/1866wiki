# Escalation Logic
Escalation keeps outcomes consistent across cases. Patterns, evasion, and tampering move offenses up tiers automatically or trigger alliance-level review.

---

## Escalation Triggers

| Trigger | Escalation | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd Minor within 14 days | → **Moderate** | Pattern of carelessness |
| 2nd Moderate within 30 days | → **Severe** | Willful disregard |
| Penalty evasion (ban/tag hopping) | → **Supermajority Review** | Often Severe + sanctions |
| Evidence tampering | → **Supermajority Review** | Likely indefinite blacklist |
| Cross-server collusion | → **Supermajority Review** | Treated as Severe |
| Refusal of diplomacy | → **Severe** | Applies to R5 or player |
| Probation violation | → **Next tier automatically** | No vote needed |
| Harboring penalized/blacklisted player | → **Alliance-level review** | Protection/rotation may be suspended |
| Alliance refuses to enforce penalties | → **Alliance-level review** | May escalate to expulsion |
| Multiple coordinated offenders | → **Alliance-level review** | Indicates leadership issue |

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities
- **Alliance:** ensure offender complies, prevent sheltering, provide proof when asked.  
- **Council/Clerks:** track recurrence, log aggravating factors, initiate escalation votes when triggers fire.***
