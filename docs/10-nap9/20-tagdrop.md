# Tag-Drop Blacklist System
Dropping tags to attack is the **only automatic blacklist rule** in NAP 9. It bypasses deliberation because it hides identity and destabilizes diplomacy.

---

## Instance One
- **48-hour ban** from all alliances; offender must stay untagged.  
- No alliance may shelter them during the ban.

---

## Instance Two
- **Automatic permanent blacklist**.  
- Overturn requires **Supermajority (7 YES)**.

---

## Harboring Rule (applies to all bans/blacklists)
If an alliance shelters a banned/blacklisted player or penalty evader:

- Immediate loss of **all NAP protection**.  
- Rotation and voting rights suspended until compliance is confirmed.  
- May trigger alliance-level sanctions or expulsion.

Protection is restored only after the player leaves and the Council confirms compliance.***
