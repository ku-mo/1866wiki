# Communication Protocols
NAP 9 runs on asynchronous coordination. Only specific channels count as **official** for governance.

---

## Platform Hierarchy
1. **NAP Discord** — authoritative for evidence, votes, announcements, and logs.  
2. **1866 Public Discord** — secondary for visibility, not binding.  
3. **In-game chat/DMs/Moments** — notification only; not governance.

If it is not in NAP Discord, it is not official.

---

## Server Time (ST) Standard
All deadlines, vote windows, penalties, evidence, and archives use **Server Time**. Missing or mismatched time zones can delay or invalidate cases.

---

## Announcement Protocol
- Binding decisions are posted in `#nap-announcements` and `#case-log`.  
- Deliberation happens in `#nap-council-chat`.  
- Amendments post to `#nap-proposals` with a notice period.  
- Associate Members can read announcements but do not vote.

---

## Communication Etiquette
- Keep messages concise; avoid inflammatory tone.  
- Do not DM Clerks evidence or attempt to lobby them.  
- Request clarifications publicly; cite sections when challenging procedure.  
- Vote text must be explicit; reactions/emojis are not votes.***
