# Governance Council
The Council is the nine-seat governing body of NAP 9. One voting representative per Full Member alliance; Alternates vote only when the primary is unavailable.

---

## Mandate
- Enforce the Covenant and land rules.  
- Classify offenses and vote on penalties.  
- Manage membership changes and expulsions.  
- Amend the Covenant via the formal process.

Council members **judge outcomes**; Clerks **run procedure**.

---

## Quorum & Thresholds
- **Quorum:** 5 of 9 votes (YES / NO / ABSTAIN).  
- **Simple Majority:** 5 YES.  
- **Supermajority:** 7 YES (blacklists, expulsions, rotation removal, amendments, overturning severe penalties).

Abstain counts toward quorum. Absence does not.

---

## Participation Requirements
In any 14-day window:

- Miss **2 Emergency votes** OR **3 total votes** → Council review.  
- Possible outcomes (Simple Majority): rotation suspension, downgrade to Associate, temporary loss of voting rights.

Votes close early if all 9 votes are submitted before the window ends.

---

## Conflict of Interest
- Recuse (or ABSTAIN) if your alliance is the offender, victim, or targeted by the amendment/penalty.  
- Alternate votes instead; if both are conflicted, the seat auto-ABSTAINS.

---

## Council Conduct
- Keep discussion brief, factual, and professional.  
- Do not pressure Clerks or bypass evidence rules.  
- In-game chat is **not binding**; only NAP Discord channels are official.  
- Respect vote locks—votes cannot be edited once cast.***
