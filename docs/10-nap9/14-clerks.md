# Clerkship System (Neutral Operators)
Clerks operate the system so the Council can deliberate. They are **neutral, non-voting** administrators drawn from three different NAP 9 alliances (never R5s).

---

## Responsibilities
- Validate evidence and request fixes.  
- Assign Case IDs and post to `#case-log`.  
- Open/close vote windows and record results.  
- Maintain archives (cases, appeals, amendments).  
- Track penalty enforcement and compliance pings.  
- Provide procedural answers; never recommend outcomes.

---

## Eligibility
- Member of a NAP 9 alliance but **not** the R5/primary delegate.  
- No penalties in the last 30 days; responsive and impartial.  
- Discord-literate; not sharing an alliance with another Clerk.

---

## Appointment
1. Announcement in NAP + 1866 Discord.  
2. Applicants submit IGN, alliance, experience, and impartiality statement.  
3. Optional R5 conflict checks.  
4. Voting: 16h screening vote → 24h final vote. **Simple Majority (≥5 YES)** seats the top candidates until three Clerk roles are filled.

---

## Term, Suspension, Removal
- **Term:** 90 days.  
- **Removal (Simple Majority):** inactivity (3 missed cases in 14 days), misconduct, evidence tampering, becoming R5, sharing alliance with another Clerk, loss of neutrality.  
- **Suspension:** while under investigation; may not act as Clerk.  
- **Vacancy:** triggers a Clerk appointment vote within 48 hours.

---

## Emergency Coverage
If no Clerk responds within 4 hours during an urgent matter, Council may call an **8-hour Emergency Temporary Clerk vote**. The temporary Clerk may:

- Create/log cases and open vote windows.  
- They may **not** archive, manage appeals, or exercise broader discretion unless the vote text says so.
