# Voting System (Asynchronous)
Votes are built for global time zones. Clerks open and close windows; Council votes.

---

## Quorum & Thresholds
- **Quorum:** 5 of 9 votes (YES / NO / ABSTAIN).  
- **Simple Majority:** 5 YES.  
- **Supermajority:** 7 YES (required for blacklists, expulsions, rotation removal, amendments, overturning Severe penalties).

Abstain counts toward quorum. Votes are **locked** once posted.

---

## Vote Windows
- **24h (Long):** amendments, appeals, blacklists, alliance expulsion, rotation changes.  
- **16h (Standard):** Moderate penalties, diplomacy rulings, routine decisions.  
- **8h (Emergency):** active attacks, land emergencies, tag-drop incidents, urgent ceasefires, forced L6 seizures.

Votes close early if all 9 votes are submitted. Missing votes are **ABSENT** (not NO).

---

## Participation Enforcement
In any 14 days:

- Miss **2 Emergency votes** OR **3 total votes** → review.  
- Potential outcomes (Simple Majority): rotation suspension, downgrade to Associate, temporary loss of voting rights.

Participation resets after 14 days.

---

## Early Completion & Irregularities
- If quorum is met early, the window still runs unless all 9 votes are in.  
- Attempting to change a vote or double-vote is invalid; original vote stands.  
- Delegates vote only with authority; Alternates vote when primary is unavailable or conflicted.***
