# Title 1 — Membership {#t1}

:::info Basics
> NAP 9 divides all alliances into three categories.  
> Each state carries specific rights, protections, and obligations.  
> No alliance may occupy more than one membership state simultaneously.
:::
---

## ⚖️ Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Alliance** | A cohesive player organization operating under a single in-game tag. |
| **Full Member** | An eligible alliance formally seated within the top nine by weekly power ranking and bound under the NAP 9 Covenant. |
| **Associate Member** | A non-voting alliance that voluntarily joins NAP 9 for protection and stability, but does not possess voting privileges. |
| **Non-Member** | Any alliance or entity not recognized or protected under NAP 9 governance. |
| **Council** | The nine-seat governing body composed of the Full Members’ representatives. |
| **Clerk** | A neutral procedural officer responsible for verification, recordkeeping, and evidence management. |

---

## 🔵 Article I — Full Members (NAP 9) {#t1-art1}

:::note Summary
Full Members form the governing core of Server 1866.  
They possess voting authority and full protection under NAP 9.
:::

### Section I — Eligibility {#t1-art1-sec1}
An alliance qualifies for Full Membership when it:
1. Ranks within the **top nine alliances** by weekly power metrics, skipping ineligible alliances,
2. Publicly affirms and abides by the entirety of this Covenant,
3. Is in positive standing with the Council

### Section II — Representation {#t1-art1-sec2}
Each Full Member must designate:
- **One (1) Voting Representative**, typically the R5 or a delegated R4.  
- **One (1) Alternate**, empowered to vote only in the Representative’s absence.

Both representatives must maintain active communication on the official NAP Discord.

### Section III — Rights & Privileges {#t1-art1-sec3}
- Full NAP protection for lands the alliance currently holds.  
- Eligibility for **Council voting**.  
- Authority to propose amendments and motions.  
- Access to all governance channels and records.

### Section IV — Duties & Obligations {#t1-art1-sec4}
- Participate in all votes within the designated window.  
- Enforce penalties imposed upon their members.  
- Maintain conduct consistent with Council standards.  
- Uphold evidence integrity and respect Clerk neutrality.  
- Comply with land-related rulings issued by Council.

### Section V — Loss of Full Membership {#t1-art1-sec5}
Loss occurs automatically or by Council action if:
1. The alliance fails eligibility requirements defined in [Section I](#t1-art1-sec1); or  
2. Fails to participate in governance according to participation requirements; or  
3. Violates or refuses to enforce Council rulings.  

Vacant seats are filled by the next eligible alliance in descending power order.

### Section VI — Rank-Based Promotion and Demotion {#t1-art1-sec6}
If a Full Member alliance falls below the eligible top nine ranking, the following automatic transitions occur upon Clerk certification of the weekly ranking:

1. The **highest-ranked eligible Associate Member** is immediately promoted to Full Member status and assumes the vacated Council seat.  
2. The displaced Full Member is immediately reassigned to **Associate Member** status and occupies the newly vacated Associate slot.

No vote, petition, or discretionary action is required for these changes.

#### Eligibility for Promotion
An Associate Member is eligible for promotion only if it:
- is not under penalty, sanction, or investigation;  
- is not harboring banned or blacklisted players;  
- maintains an active R5 or designated representative;  
- has not opted out within the previous seven (7) days;  
- is in good standing under all NAP 9 requirements.

If the highest-ranked Associate is ineligible, the next highest-ranked eligible Associate is selected.

#### Associate Pool Empty
If no Associate Members exist, the displaced Full Member becomes a **Priority Associate Member**, temporarily exceeding the Associate cap.  

A Priority Associate Member:
- possesses all Associate rights and obligations,  
- does not count toward the Associate cap, and  
- is placed into the first available Associate slot once a vacancy occurs.

## 🟩 Article II — Associate Members {#t1-art2}

:::note Definition
Associate Members are non-voting participants under NAP 9 protection.  
They benefit from server stability while remaining outside the Council.
:::

### Section I — Eligibility {#t1-art2-sec1}
To be considered for Associate status, an alliance must:
- Publicly accept all provisions of the NAP 9 Covenant.  
- Maintain an active R5 or appointed representative.  
- Be free of current sanctions, investigations, or blacklisted members.  
- Not have opted out of NAP 9 within the past seven (7) days.

### Section II — Capacity {#t1-art2-sec2}
The total number of Associate Members shall **never exceed seven (7)**, excluding any Priority Associate Members created under Title 1, Article I, Section VI.

Vacancies are filled on a **first-come, first-served** basis as verified by timestamp of petition.

### Section III — Admission Procedure {#t1-art2-sec3}
1. **Petition Submission**: The R5 posts in  
   `NAP Discord → #associate-applications`, including:  
   - Alliance Name & Tag  
   - Applicant's In-Game Name  
   - Statement of acceptance of this Covenant  
   - Agreement to honor evidence and penalty procedures  
2. **Clerk Verification**: A Clerk confirms eligibility and available capacity.  
3. **Automatic Admission**: If compliant and within capacity, the alliance gains Associate status immediately upon Clerk validation.  
4. **Queue Placement**: If capacity is full, the petitioning alliance enters the official waiting queue in timestamp order.

### Section IV — Rights & Limitations {#t1-art2-sec4}
- NAP protection applies only to lands the alliance currently holds up to Level 5.  
- May submit evidence and attend Council sessions (non-voting).  
- No Council vote.  
- Must adhere to NAP 9 rulings and enforcement.

### Section V — Opt-Out or Removal {#t1-art2-sec5}
**Voluntary Opt-Out:**  
- Achieved by public declaration in `#associate-announcements`.  
- Causes immediate loss of all protections and a seven-day (7) cooldown before re-application.  

**Involuntary Removal (Simple Majority):**  
Grounds include:  
- Repeated non-response to governance communication;  
- Refusal to honor penalties or rulings;  
- Harboring banned or blacklisted players;  
- Interference with governance operations.  

Vacancies created by removal are filled automatically from the waiting queue.

## 🟥 Article III — Non-Members {#t1-art3}

:::note Non-Members
Non-Members exist entirely outside the NAP 9 framework and enjoy **no protection or procedural rights**.
:::

### Section I — Status {#t1-art3-sec1}
Any alliance not recognized as a Full or Associate Member is a Non-Member.

### Section II — Restrictions {#t1-art3-sec2}
- No NAP protection or access to evidence procedures.  
- No right to petition or appeal before the Council.  
- Lands they hold are outside NAP protection and may be contested without NAP penalties.

---

## 🟦 Article IV — Transitions of Status {#t1-art4}

| Trigger Event | Resulting Change | Required Action |
|-------------------------------|------------------------------|------------------------------|
| Drop from Eligible Top 9 | Full Member becomes Associate; highest-ranked eligible Associate becomes Full | Automatic per Title 1, Art. I, Sec. VI |
| Voluntary Opt-Out | Loss of all protection; 7-day cooldown | Clerk logs and announces |
| Expulsion (Supermajority Vote) | Forced removal; 30-day re-entry ban | Clerk records sanction |
| Reinstatement | Simple Majority after petition | Must resolve prior violations |

---

## 🧾 Article V — Interpretation {#t1-art5}

Disputes regarding membership interpretation fall under the authority of the **NAP 9 Governance Council**, whose decisions are final and binding.
