# Membership Tiers
NAP 9 recognizes **three states**. Your state determines protection, voting, and land rights.

---

## 🔵 Full Members (NAP 9)
- **Who:** Top 9 alliances by weekly power ranking that opt into NAP 9.  
- **Representation:** 1 Voting Representative + 1 Alternate.  
- **Rights:** Full NAP protection, L1–L6 rights, Council vote, rotation eligibility.  
- **Expectations:** Vote on time, honor penalties, enforce land rules.

---

## 🟩 Associate Members (Max 7)
- **Who:** Opt-in alliances seeking protection without voting.  
- **Rights:** L1–L5 protection, may submit evidence, may attend meetings.  
- **Restrictions:** No Council vote, no L6 rights, no rotation eligibility.  
- **Cap:** Strictly limited to **7 alliances**.

### Admission (First-Come, First-Serve)
1. R5 posts petition in `NAP Discord → #associate-applications` with alliance name, R5 + Discord ID, and acceptance of NAP rules.  
2. **Clerk verifies**: not blacklisted, not harboring blacklisted players, meets eligibility, slot available.  
3. If space is open, admission is **automatic**—no vote required. If full, alliance enters the waiting queue by timestamp.

### Loss or Removal
- Voluntary opt-out → immediate loss of all protections and 7-day cooldown to reapply.  
- Removal by **Simple Majority** for non-response, refusal to follow rulings, interference, or harboring blacklisted players. Vacancies fill from the queue.

---

## 🟥 Non-Members
- **No NAP protection** and **no governance rights**.  
- Cannot submit evidence or create cases.  
- **L6 must be released immediately**; NAP may seize if not.  
- L1–L5 may be held but are unprotected—others may take them without penalty.

---

## Status Changes at a Glance
- Drop from Top 9 → lose Full seat; next ranked alliance may enter.  
- Opt-out → protections end instantly; L6 must drop.  
- Expulsion → Supermajority vote; mirrors opt-out consequences plus 30-day rights loss.  
- Reinstatement → petition + Simple Majority + compliance with outstanding rulings.***
