# Evidence Requirements
A case does not exist until evidence is posted and validated. **All evidence must be submitted to `NAP Discord → #evidence`.**

---

## Required Format
- Screenshot(s) or video (uncropped when possible).  
- Server Time visible when possible.  
- Offender(s) + affected players clearly identified.  
- Short description (≤3 sentences) and damage summary if relevant.  
- Diplomacy attempt logs when the rule requires it.

---

## Invalid Evidence
Evidence is rejected if it is:

- edited or manipulated  
- cropped to hide context or timestamps  
- unverifiable or hearsay  
- Discord DMs or private submissions  
- AI-generated images  
- missing required detail after correction window

Tampering triggers Severe penalties and may go to Supermajority review.

---

## Correction Window
If evidence is incomplete, a Clerk will list missing items. The submitter has **6 hours** to correct (Clerks may extend to 12–24 hours for time zones or urgent clarity). Missing the window invalidates the case.

---

## Archiving
Clerks store:

- Case ID and classification  
- Evidence media and summaries  
- Timestamps and penalties  
- Restitution/compliance notes  
- Appeals (if any)

Archives must be accessible to all Full Member representatives.***
