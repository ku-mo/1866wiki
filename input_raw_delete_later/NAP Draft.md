# 🛡️ NAP 9 Governance Covenant  
_Server 1866 — Alliance Governance Framework_

> [!summary]+ **Summary**  
> NAP 9 establishes a functional, enforceable, and tightly scoped governance system for Server 1866, reducing the governing body to nine alliances and formalizing communication, penalties, procedures, evidence standards, and voting rules.  


---

# 📘 SECTION I — Purpose & Authority

> [!info] **Purpose**  
> - Create a smaller, more responsive governing body  
> - Standardize penalties & definitions  
> - Ensure synchronized cross-platform communication  
> - Maintain fairness, enforceability, and stability  

> [!note] **Scope**  
> Applies to:  
> - All NAP 9 Full Member alliances  
> - All Associate Member alliances  
> - All players on Server 1866  

> [!danger] **Authority**  
> The **NAP 9 Governance Council** (“the Council”) is the *sole* authority empowered to interpret, enforce, and amend this Covenant.

---

# 🧱 SECTION II — Membership Tiers

## 🟦 Full Members (NAP 9)

Full Members = **Top 9 Alliances** by weekly power ranking.  

Each alliance appoints:
- **1 Voting Representative** (R5 or delegate)  
- **1 Alternate** (R4 or officer)

> [!tip] Full Members hold voting and rotation rights.

---

## 🟩 Associate Members

> [!info] **What is an Associate Member?**  
> Associate Members are non–NAP 9 alliances that voluntarily join the NAP system for protection and community stability.  
> They do **not** vote and do **not** participate in land rotations, but they *are* protected by the NAP and may submit evidence and attend meetings.

Associate Members:
- Opt in voluntarily  
- Receive NAP protection  
- May submit evidence  
- May attend meetings (non-voting)  
- Are limited to a **maximum of seven (7)** Associate Member alliances at any given time  

> [!warning]  
> Associate Members **do not** have voting or rotation rights.  
> Once the limit of **7 Associate Members** is reached, additional alliances may only join if a vacancy opens.
> Membership is in order of application.

---

## 🟩 Associate Member Admission

### 📝 Eligibility Requirements

To become an Associate Member, an alliance must:
- Publicly affirm acceptance of the full NAP 9 Covenant  
- Maintain an active R5 or designated representative for communication  
- Agree to honor NAP rulings, evidence procedures, penalties, and Council decisions  
- Not be harboring any banned or blacklisted players  
- Not be under active penalties or sanctions  
- Not be attempting to bypass prior expulsion or disciplinary action

### 🟦 Admission Process (First-Come, First-Serve)

> [!tip] **Capacity Limit**  
> Associate Membership is capped at **7 alliances total**.  
> Admission follows a strict **first-come-first-serve** queue.

**Step 1 — Petition Submission**  
The R5 of the alliance posts a petition in:  
`NAP Discord → #associate-applications`  
Petition must include:
- Alliance name & tag  
- R5 name & Discord ID  
- Confirmation of NAP acceptance  
- Agreement to follow evidence procedures  
- Agreement to comply with all Council rulings  

**Step 2 — Clerk Verification**  
A Clerk verifies:
- The alliance is not blacklisted  
- The alliance is not harboring blacklisted players  
- The alliance is not currently under investigation  
- The alliance meets eligibility requirements  
- There is available space (≤7 Associate Members)

If verification passes, the Clerk logs the petition.

**Step 3 — Automatic Admission**  
If the cap has not been reached:
- The alliance becomes an Associate Member **immediately upon Clerk validation**  
- The Clerk updates the Associate Member registry  
- No Council vote is required  

> [!success]  
> **Associate Membership is automatic** unless the cap is full or the verification process finds a disqualifying condition.

---

### 🔄 Membership Queue (If Full)

If all 7 slots are filled:
- Additional alliances are placed in a **waiting queue** based on timestamp of petition  
- When a vacancy opens (opt-out, expulsion, inactivity removal), the next alliance in queue is automatically admitted  
- The Clerk maintains and posts queue order

### ❌ Grounds for Denial
A petition is denied if:
- The alliance is currently sanctioned  
- The alliance has an unresolved investigation  
- The alliance is harboring banned/blacklisted players  
- The R5 refuses to follow NAP communications or evidence standards  
- The alliance previously opted out within the last 7 days  

### 🚪 Opt-Out of Associate Membership
An alliance may opt out at any time with a public declaration in:
`NAP Discord → #associate-announcements`

Consequences:
- Immediate loss of protections  
- Removal from queue (if reapplying)  
- 7-day re-entry cooldown  

### ⚠️ Removal of Associate Member Status
The Council may remove an Associate Member by **simple majority** for:
- repeated non-responsiveness  
- refusal to follow NAP penalty rulings  
- interfering with NAP governance  
- harboring blacklisted players  

This creates a vacancy for the next alliance in queue.

# 👥 SECTION III — Governance Council

> [!info] **Council Size**  
> **9 voting seats**, one per Full Member alliance.

## Quorum

> [!quote] **Asynchronous Quorum**  
> Quorum is achieved when **5 of 9** voting representatives cast a vote (YES / NO / ABSTAIN) within the active vote window.

## Voting Thresholds

- **Simple Majority:** 5 YES  
- **Supermajority:** 7 YES  

> [!danger] **Supermajority required for:**  
> - Permanent blacklists  
> - Permanent bans  
> - Alliance expulsion  
> - Rotation removal  
> - Amendments  
> - Overturning severe penalties

# 🗂️ SECTION IV — Clerkship System (Neutral Operators)

> [!important]  
> NAP 9 appoints **three (3)** Clerks — the *only* neutral operators.  
> Clerks cannot vote, must each be from **different NAP 9 alliances**, and **cannot** be R5s.

## Clerk Responsibilities

- Log evidence  
- Assign case numbers  
- Open and close vote windows  
- Maintain #case-log  
- Verify timestamps  
- Post results  
- Maintain archives  

## Eligibility

Clerks must:
- Be active members of a **NAP 9 alliance**  
- Not be the R5 of their alliance  
- Be penalty-free and responsive  
- Be conflict-free  
- Be Discord literate  

## Application & Appointment

> [!done]+ **Application Steps**  
> 1. Announcement posted in NAP & 1866 Discords  
> 2. Applicants submit:
>     - In-game Name  
>     - Current NAP 9 Alliance  
>     - Any Relevant Experience  
>     - Impartiality Statement  
> 3. Conflict checks conducted voluntarily by R5s  
>     - (e.g., evidence of bias, history of disputes, alliance internal politics, active penalties)

> [!check]+ **Appointment Process**  
> - **Step 1:** 16-hour screening vote (Simple Majority)  
> - **Step 2:** 24-hour final vote (Simple Majority ≥5 YES)  
> - Top eligible candidates are selected until all three Clerk positions are filled  

## Term & Removal

- Term length: **90 days**  
- Removal grounds:
    - Inactivity (3 missed cases within 14 days)  
    - Misconduct or evidence tampering  
    - Joining or becoming R5 of an alliance with an existing Clerk  
    - Majority Council vote

# 🗳️ SECTION VII — Asynchronous Voting System

## Vote Windows

> [!note]+ **🕒 Long Votes — 24 Hours**  
> Used for:  
> - Amendments  
> - Appeals  
> - Blacklistings  
> - Alliance expulsion  
> - Rotation changes  

> [!note]+ **🕓 Standard Votes — 16 Hours**  
> Used for:  
> - Moderate penalties  
> - Diplomacy rulings  
> - Routine decisions  

> [!note]+ **🕗 Emergency Votes — 8 Hours**  
> Used for:  
> - Ongoing attacks  
> - Land emergencies  
> - Urgent ceasefires  
> - Cross-server threats  

## Quorum
> [!quote]  
> Quorum = **5 of 9 votes** submitted before the window closes.

## Participation Failures

If an alliance misses:
- **2 emergency votes**, or  
- **3 total votes in 14 days**,  

Council must review:
- rotating them out of voting,  
- downgrading to Associate Member,  
- suspending rotation rights.

# ⚖️ SECTION VIII — Offense Categories & Penalties

This section defines clear offense categories, their operational meaning, required evidence, escalation logic, and mandatory penalties.  

Use this as the **primary Council reference** during case evaluation.

---

# 🟨 MINOR OFFENSES

> [!summary] **Definition**  
> Low-impact, accidental, or isolated violations with no malicious intent and minimal loss.  
> A Minor Offense becomes Moderate if repeated within 14 days.

## 📝 Examples

- Accidental tile hit  
- Unintentional troop contact  
- Mild chat misconduct  
- Minor rotation disruption  
- Failure to respond in diplomacy within a reasonable window  

## 📄 Required Evidence

- Timestamped screenshot/video  
- Short description (<3 sentences)  
- Damage summary (if any)  
- Confirmation of accidental nature (if applicable)

## 🟩 Penalties (Standard)

> [!tip] **Default outcome unless Council votes otherwise**

- Formal warning (logged by Clerk)  
- Restitution for any loss  
- Possible discretionary **24–48 hour Probation Period**  
    - No non-capitol PvP  
    - Any violation during probation → automatic Moderate Offense  

## ⚖️ Mitigating Factors

- Immediate apology / restitution  
- Self-reporting  
- Clean record (30 days)

## 🔺 Aggravating Factors

- Repeated minor misbehavior  
- Failure to respond  
- Casual negligence affecting others

---

# 🟧 MODERATE OFFENSES

> [!warning] **Definition**  
> Meaningful disruption, repeated violations, or harmful actions lacking explicit malice but causing measurable impact.

## 📝 Examples

- Repeated task/truck plundering  
- Tile/base aggression without tag drop  
- Pattern of diplomacy refusal  
- Disrupting L6 operations  
- Violating probation terms  

## 📄 Required Evidence

- Timestamped media  
- Pattern or repetition demonstrated  
- Damage or loss quantification  
- Proof of attempted diplomacy (R5→R5)  
- Clerk case log entry

## 🟧 Penalties (Standard)

> [!note] These do **not** require a supermajority.

- Mandatory restitution  
- **48–72 Hour Combat Restriction**
    - No offensive actions except agreed Capitol combat  
- **Temporary Rotation Suspension** (individual or alliance)  
- Placement on **Moderate Watchlist** for 14 days

## ⚖️ Mitigating Factors

- Rapid diplomacy compliance  
- Full restitution before Council vote  

## 🔺 Aggravating Factors

- Repeat within 30 days  
- Defiance, evasion, or deletion of evidence  
- Coordinated behavior by multiple players

---

# 🟥 SEVERE OFFENSES

> [!danger] **Definition**  
> Actions showing intent, high impact, coordinated aggression, destabilization, or evasion of penalties.  
> 
> Severe Offenses include misconduct serious enough to endanger server integrity.

## 📝 Examples

- Coordinated attacks on NAP alliances  
- L6 land theft / obstruction  
- Cross-server collusion against 1866  
- Harassment or targeted toxic conduct  
- Penalty evasion (ban evasion, tag hopping)  
- Evidence tampering or manipulation  
- Repeated Moderate Offenses without remediation  

## 📄 Required Evidence

- Verified timestamped media  
- Corroborating sources  
- Damage or impact summary  
- Diplomacy attempt logs  
- Clerk verification & authenticity check  

---

## 🟥 Penalties — Simple Majority (≥5 YES)

> [!warning] These apply to **serious but not existential threats**.

- **72-hour ban** from any alliance - must remain untagged.
- **7-day ban** from any NAP alliance.
- Mandatory restitution  
- Offender placed under **Severe Review**  

---

## 🟥 Penalties — Supermajority (≥7 YES)

> [!danger] Reserved for destabilizing behavior, repeated offenses, or proven malicious intent.

- **Permanent Blacklist**  
- **Alliance Sanctions**, including:  
    - rotation removal  
    - suspension of NAP protections  
    - temporary demotion to Associate Member (up to 30 days)
- **Indefinite Rotation Removal**
- **Indefinite Alliance Expulsion** from NAP

# 🗃️ SECTION XI — Case Processing

This section defines the mandatory procedure for reporting, validating, documenting, and advancing cases through the NAP 9 governance system.  
No case may proceed without following these steps exactly.

## 🟦 1. Case Workflow Overview

> [!note]+ **Three-Step Case System (Mandatory)**  
> **1. Notification** — Any platform, initial alert only  
> **2. Evidence Submission** — Must be submitted to **#evidence**  
> **3. Clerk Log Entry** — Clerk assigns Case ID in **#case-log**  

Council deliberation may **only begin after Step 3** is completed.

## 🟦 2. Step One — Notification

A case may be **initiated by anyone** through:
- World chat  
- Alliance chat  
- Private DM (in-game or Discord)  
- Moments feed  
- Public or private Discord messages  

>[!warning] Warning
>Notification does **not** create a case.  
>It only alerts R5s or Clerks that an incident has occurred.

Acceptable content at this stage:
- Basic description of event  
- Identifying involved players  
- “Evidence incoming” statements  
- Time and location reference  

Unacceptable content at this stage:
- Requests for punishment  
- Diplomatic escalation  
- Edited media  
- Accusations without intent to submit evidence  

Once evidence is ready, the notifier proceeds to Step Two.

## 🟦 3. Step Two — Evidence Submission

> [!info]  
> Evidence must be submitted to **NAP Discord → #evidence** for a case to formally exist.

Required contents in evidence submission:
- Screenshot(s) or video  
- Visible Server Time when possible  
- Names of offender(s) and affected parties  
- Short structured description (≤3 sentences)  
- Any relevant diplomatic logs (proof of contact attempts)  

Optional but helpful:
- Loss/damage estimates  
- Additional witness confirmation  
- Sequence of events  

Evidence is considered valid when:
- It is complete  
- It is unedited or unmanipulated  
- It is timestamped or corroborated  
- It aligns with NAP 9 evidence standards  

If evidence is incomplete:
- A Clerk will request specific clarifications  
- The submitter has **6 hours** to amend  
- Failure to amend results in case invalidation  

## 🟦 4. Step Three — Clerk Log Entry

Once valid evidence is posted, a Clerk who verbally takes ownership of the case must:

1. **Assign a Unique Case ID**
2. Add the case to **#case-log** with:
    - Offender(s)  
    - Offense category (preliminary)  
    - Evidence summary  
    - Timestamp of submission  
    - Reporting alliance  
3. Confirm that case is now active  
4. Notify Council representatives via appropriate tags  

> [!warning]  
> The Council may **not** deliberate, respond, vote, classify, or apply penalties **until this step is completed**.

If multiple Clerks are online:
- The first Clerk to acknowledge the case handles logging  
- Other Clerks verify accuracy  

If no Clerk is available within 2 hours:
- Any R5 may ping the Clerks directly  
- Failure of all Clerks within 8 hours → emergency temporary Clerk appointment vote  

## 🟦 5. Council Activation

Once Step Three is complete:
- Council may discuss the case  
- Clerks may open a vote window  
- Investigations may begin
- Diplomacy may be initiated
- Alliance leaders may issue statements

Premature Council action prior to Step Three is invalid.

## 🟦 6. Case Classification

During initial review, the clerk allocated to the case will provisionally classify the case as:
- **Minor Offense**  
- **Moderate Offense**  
- **Severe Offense**  
- **Exceptional Case** (requires special ruling or amendment review)  

Classification determines:
- Evidence threshold  
- Penalty severity  
- Vote window type  
- Appeal eligibility  

Clerks update the Case ID with its classification tag.

## 🟦 7. Consolidated Cases

If multiple incidents occur involving:
- the same offender, or  
- the same event window, or  
- the same evidence bundle  

Clerks may consolidate these into a single Case ID, provided:
- All involved incidents are clearly linked  
- Council agrees consolidation improves clarity  
- No party loses procedural rights  

Consolidation is noted in #case-log.

## 🟦 8. Duplicate or Frivolous Cases

Clerks may mark a case as **Duplicate** if:
- It references an already-opened investigation  
- It contains no new evidence  

Clerks may mark a case as **Frivolous** if:
- It lacks substance  
- It is submitted in bad faith  
- It is retaliatory without evidence  

Frivolous cases may trigger:
- A warning  
- Probation  
- Escalation to Moderate Offense (if malicious)

## 🟦 9. Case Closure

A case is closed when:
- Penalty is issued  
- Case is dismissed  
- Appeal period expires  
- Appeal is processed  
- Offender leaves the server permanently  

Clerks must:
- Mark the case as **Resolved**, **Dismissed**, or **Archived**  
- Record penalty start/end timestamps  
- Update offender history for escalation tracking  
- Move the case to **#case-archive**  

## 🟦 10. Summary Table

| Step | Name | Action | Who Performs It |
|------|-------|---------|------------------|
| 1 | Notification | Initial alert on any platform | Anyone |
| 2 | Evidence Submission | Official submission to #evidence | Reporting party |
| 3 | Clerk Log Entry | Case ID assigned; logged in #case-log | Clerk |
| 4 | Council Activation | Discussion + investigation | Council |
| 5 | Case Classification | Minor / Moderate / Severe | Council or Investigative Officer |
| 6 | Resolution | Penalty, dismissal, or escalation | Council |
| 7 | Archival | Case closed + stored | Clerk |

# 🔁 SECTION XII — Appeals

The Appeals process provides a structured, time-bound method for challenging penalties. All appeals must follow the procedures below to be considered valid.

## 🟦 1. Eligibility for Appeal

> [!info]  
> Only penalties with a **duration greater than 48 hours** are eligible for appeal.

Eligible for appeal:

- 72-hour bans  
- 7-day bans  
- Temporary loss of NAP protection  
- Rotation suspensions longer than 48 hours  
- Alliance-level sanctions  
- Severe Offense penalties  
- Permanent blacklists (appealable once)

Not eligible for appeal:

- Warnings  
- 24–48 hour probations  
- Restitution-only outcomes  
- Minor Offense rulings  
- Tag-drop Instance One penalties (48 hours)

A permanent blacklist may be appealed **once** and only with new evidence.

## 🟦 2. Filing an Appeal

> [!tip]  
> Appeals must be filed within **48 hours** of the penalty being issued.

To file an appeal, the offender or their R5 must submit the following in:

**NAP Discord → #appeals**

Required appeal components:

- Case Number (from Clerk log)  
- Offender’s IGN and Alliance  
- Penalty being appealed  
- Grounds for appeal (choose one):
    - Incorrect offense classification  
    - Procedural error  
    - Misinterpretation of evidence  
    - New exculpatory evidence  
    - Disproportionate penalty  
- Supporting screenshots or statements  

Clerks will reject appeals that are late, incomplete, or ineligible.

## 🟦 3. Clerk Intake and Validation

Upon receipt, Clerks must:

1. Confirm eligibility  
2. Validate the appeal contains all required components  
3. Assign an **Appeal ID** (e.g., AP-14-03)  
4. Enter the appeal into #case-log  
5. Notify all Council representatives  

If the appeal is incomplete, the Clerk returns it with a list of missing items.  
The appellant has **6 hours** to correct errors before the appeal is void.

## 🟦 4. Investigative Review

> [!info]  
> The Investigative Officer has **24 hours** to complete the review.

The review includes:

- Re-examining the original evidence  
- Evaluating appeal justification  
- Checking timestamps  
- Identifying procedural errors  
- Assessing any newly submitted evidence  
- Contacting involved parties if needed  

The Investigative Officer posts a neutral summary to #case-log, including:

- Recommended classification (uphold, reduce, overturn)  
- Noted mitigating or aggravating factors  

## 🟦 5. Council Vote

After the investigative summary is posted:

> [!warning]  
> A **24-hour Long-Window Vote** begins immediately.

Voting options:

- **YES** — Grant appeal  
- **NO** — Deny appeal  
- **ABSTAIN** — Neutral (counts for quorum)

Quorum: **5 votes** submitted within the voting window.

## 🟦 6. Appeal Thresholds

> [!info]  
> These thresholds determine the outcome of the appeal.

- **Simple Majority (≥5 YES)** → Penalty is **reduced**  
- **Supermajority (≥7 YES)** → Penalty is **overturned**  
- If neither threshold is met → Appeal fails  

Penalty reductions may include:

- Shorter ban  
- Removal of rotation suspension  
- Reduced offense classification  

Overturning results in:

- Full reversal  
- Removal of the case from active disciplinary status  
- Public corrective notice in #nap-announcements  

## 🟦 7. Restrictions During Appeal

The offender must **continue serving the penalty** during the appeal process.

No alliance may:

- Shelter the offender  
- Alter or delay enforcement  
- Interfere with compliance  

> [!danger]  
> Violating a penalty during an active appeal results in **automatic dismissal** of the appeal and **escalation to a Severe Offense**.

## 🟦 8. Finalization and Archival

After the Council vote, Clerks must:

- Update the case file with the final outcome  
- Record vote totals  
- Modify penalty duration if reduced  
- Move the appeal to the “Resolved Appeals” archive  
- Publish a summary in #nap-announcements  

## 🟦 9. Appeal Limitations

- A player or alliance may appeal **one penalty per 30 days**  
- Permanent blacklists may be appealed **once only**  
- Appeals cannot challenge:
    - Valid evidence  
    - Council quorum rules  
    - Amendment decisions  
    - Previously denied appeals after the 30-day window  

## 🟦 10. Summary Table

| Stage | Action | Time Limit |
|-------|--------|------------|
| 1 | File appeal | 48 hours |
| 2 | Clerk validation | Immediate (or +6 hours correction window) |
| 3 | Investigative review | 24 hours |
| 4 | Council vote | 24 hours |
| 5 | Appeal outcome | Immediate after vote |
| 6 | Archive update | Within 6 hours |

# 📝 SECTION XIV — Amendments

> [!summary]  
> This section defines how the NAP 9 Covenant may be amended, including proposal submission, review, voting, adoption, and archival requirements.

## 🧩 1. Eligibility to Propose Amendments

Any of the following may propose an amendment:
- A Voting Representative (R5 or their designated delegate)  
- A Clerk (procedural or structural recommendations only)  
- A coalition of **three (3)** Associate Member alliances acting jointly  

> [!warning]  
> Individual players **cannot** propose amendments directly.  
> They may petition their R5, who may sponsor the amendment.

## 🗂️ 2. Amendment Proposal Requirements

A valid amendment proposal must include:
- A clear **Section & Subsection reference** (what is being modified)  
- The **full text** of the proposed revision  
- A **justification statement** (1–5 sentences)  
- Whether the amendment is:  
  - **Additive** (adds new content)  
  - **Revisory** (replaces existing content)  
  - **Repeal** (removes content)  
  - **Structural** (changes governance procedures)  
- Whether the amendment includes a **sunset clause**  
- Whether it is intended to be **retroactive** (default: no)  
- Whether it requires **immediate application** or **scheduled activation**

> [!tip]  
> Proposals missing required components are rejected by the Clerk and must be resubmitted.

## ⏳ 3. Notice & Posting Requirements

The proposing party must submit the amendment to:
- **NAP Discord → #nap-proposals**  
- **Clerkship**, via direct mention or designated form  

Upon receipt, Clerks must:
1. Assign an **Amendment ID** (e.g., *A14-07*)  
2. Post it in:  
   - **#nap-proposals** (discussion allowed)  
   - **#nap-announcements** (read-only)  
3. Record it in the **Amendment Archive**

> [!info]  
> All amendments require a **24-hour Notice Period** before voting can begin.

## 🧭 4. Discussion Period (Optional, but Normal)

During the Notice Period:
- R5s may discuss, request clarifications, or propose micro-edits  
- Clerks may issue formatting or procedural corrections  
- Associate Members may comment (non-binding)

If substantive changes are made:
- Clock **resets**, requiring a new 24-hour Notice Period  

## 🗳️ 5. Voting Procedure

After the Notice Period:
- Clerks open a **24-hour Long-Window Vote**  
- Quorum applies (minimum 5/9 votes cast)  
- Passage requires **Supermajority (≥7 YES)**

> [!warning] No abstention may be retracted once cast.  
> Any R5 who fails to vote is counted as **ABSENT**, not NO.

## ⚖️ 6. Adoption & Activation

If passed:
- Amendment takes **immediate effect**, unless the amendment states:
  - A scheduled activation time  
  - A phased rollout  
  - A conditional activation tied to events  
  - A sunset date  

If the amendment contradicts older sections:
- The new amendment **supersedes** prior language  
- Clerks must mark superseded sections as **RETIRED** in the archive

## 🚫 7. Failure to Pass

If an amendment fails:

- It may not be reintroduced for **7 days**.
- At the agreement of **two** clerks:
	- A heavily revised version may be resubmitted immediately,
	- Minor formatting or procedural corrections that prevented it's success are allowed immediately.

## 🛑 8. Emergency Amendment Pathway

Only allowed when:
- Immediate governance action is required to prevent server harm  
- At least **5 of 9** R5s jointly invoke the emergency pathway  

Procedure:
1. Minimum **1-hour Notice Period**  
2. **8-hour Vote Window**  
3. Still requires **Supermajority (≥7 YES)**  

> [!danger]  
> Emergency amendments **expire in 30 days** unless renewed through the standard process.

## 🧱 9. Conflict of Interest Restrictions

A R5 may not vote on amendments that:
- Target their own removal  
- Target their alliance for sanction or penalty  
- Grant their alliance special privileges  

In such cases:
- Their Alternate votes instead  
- If both are conflicted, the seat abstains automatically  

## 📜 10. Clerk Archive Requirements

For every amendment:

- Clerks must store:
  
	  - Amendment ID  
	  - Text before and after revision  
	  - Vote breakdown  
	  - Activation details  
	  - Sunset/expiry (if any)  
	  - Conflict-of-interest substitutions
	    
- Archive must be accessible to all NAP 9 representatives

## 🧾 11. Amendment Formatting Standards

All amendment text must:

- Use the same heading hierarchy as the NAP  
- Use Obsidian-compatible Markdown  
- Contain emojis where appropriate for clarity  
- Avoid ambiguous terms (“may,” “should,” “suggested”)  
- Use deterministic legal language (MUST, SHALL, MUST NOT)

## 🏁 12. Final Summary

> [!info] **Amendment Workflow**
> 1. **Proposal Submitted**
> 2. **Clerk Validates & Posts**
> 3. **24-Hour Notice Period**
> 4. **24-Hour Voting Window**
> 5. **Supermajority (7 of 9 YES)**
> 6. **Amendment Activated & Archived**
