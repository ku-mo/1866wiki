# 🛡 NAP 9 — Player Quick Reference

_Server 1866_

> This is a simplified overview for everyday players.  
> For full legal text, see the **NAP 9 Governance Covenant**.

---

## 1️⃣ What is NAP 9?

NAP 9 is the **server-wide agreement** between the **top 9 alliances** on 1866.

It exists to:

- Reduce random drama and surprise attacks
    
- Keep **land and L6 rotation** stable
    
- Provide **clear rules** and **fair punishments**
    
- Make sure **evidence-based decisions** are used, not rage
    

If your alliance is in NAP 9 or an Associate Member, you are **protected** by these rules.

---

## 2️⃣ Who Is Protected?

### 🔵 Full Members (NAP 9)

- Top 9 alliances by power (that choose to be in NAP)
    
- Have voting reps on the Council
    
- Protected for **L1–L6** land
    
- Eligible for **Level 6 rotation**
    

### 🟩 Associate Members

- Extra alliances that opt into protection (max 7)
    
- Protected for **L1–L5** land
    
- **Cannot** hold Level 6 land
    
- **No voting rights**, no L6 rotation rights
    

### 🟥 Non-Members

- Not in NAP 9 and not Associate
    
- **No NAP protection**
    
- May hold L1–L5 but:
    
    - anyone can take their land
        
    - no penalties can be claimed through NAP
        
- **Cannot hold L6** – if they do, NAP 9 will remove them by force
    

If you don’t know your status: **ask your leadership.**

---

## 3️⃣ Core Player Rules (Plain Language)

### 🧱 Land & L6

- Land is considered “**settled**” – don’t randomly steal it from protected alliances
    
- **Level 6 lands**:
    
    - Only the **Top 3 alliances** (by power) get to hold them in rotation
        
    - Rotates weekly, **Sunday @ 12:00 ST**, clockwise
        
    - Alliances must temporarily drop/pick up on schedule
        
- Non-Members:
    
    - must drop any L6 immediately
        
    - have **no protection** for L1–L5 (they’re fair game)
        

---

### 🚛 Tasks, Trucks, Trains

- **Do NOT plunder tasks, trucks, or trains from 1866 players**
    
- Plundering should be done **off-server**
    
- If you hit an in-server task/truck, you’ll be expected to:
    
    - allow a **retaliatory hit**, or
        
    - send a **truck/task of equal value**, possibly from another server
        

Repeated plundering → higher-level penalties or possible blacklist.

---

### 🏠 Base & Tile Hits

- Do **not** hit bases or tiles of NAP-protected alliances in normal conditions
    
- If you are hit, your leadership should handle it through NAP, **not you personally**
    
- Tiles in **Capitol Zone** are special (see Capitol section below)
    

---

### 🎯 Capitol Zone Rules

Capitol Mud + resource tiles in the Capitol Zone are a **free combat zone**, but:

- Fighting there must be **mutually agreed** between alliances
    
- If you hit someone’s gathering tile **without agreement**, you owe:
    
    - a **return hit of equal power on your own squad** (“eye for an eye”)
        

Capitol PvP does **not** give you a right to retaliate outside Capitol.

---

### 🧨 Tag-Dropping (VERY IMPORTANT)

> Dropping alliance tags to attack is the **one thing** that automatically blacklists you.

- **1st time:**
    
    - 48-hour ban from all alliances (you must stay untagged)
        
- **2nd time:**
    
    - **Permanent NAP-wide blacklist**
        

Any alliance that shelters a banned/blacklisted tag-dropper **loses NAP protection** until they kick them.

---

### 😈 Behavior & Representation

- Don’t troll, bully, or trash the server’s name on other servers
    
- Don’t coordinate cross-server attacks on another 1866 alliance
    
- Don’t deliberately stir drama using other servers or alts
    

Serious or repeated toxic behavior could have codified consequences for you or your alliance.

---

## 4️⃣ Penalties (What Can Happen to You)

Simplified:

### 🟨 Minor Offense

Examples:

- A single accidental tile hit
    
- Small, low-impact mistake
    
- Light chat issue
    

Likely outcomes:

- Warning
    
- Make it right (restitution)
    
- Short probation (24–48h, “no non-capitol PvP”)
    

---

### 🟧 Moderate Offense

Examples:

- Repeating the same issue
    
- Repeated plundering
    
- Ignoring diplomacy
    
- Messing with L6 rotation (but not all-out theft)
    

Likely outcomes:

- 48–72h combat restriction (no offensive hits outside Capitol)
    
- Temp removal from rotation
    
- Watchlist status
    

---

### 🟥 Severe Offense

Examples:

- Coordinated attacks on a NAP alliance
    
- L6 land theft
    
- Cross-server collusion against 1866
    
- Tag-drop repeat
    
- Penalty and evidence evasion
    
- Tampering with proof
    

Likely outcomes:

- 72h+ bans
    
- 7-day bans
    
- Loss of NAP protection
    
- Permanent blacklist
    
- Alliance-level sanctions or expulsion
    

---

## 5️⃣ How to Report an Incident

If something happens:

### Step 1 — Notify leadership

Tell your R5 or officers:

- **Who** did it
    
- **What** happened
    
- **When** it happened (Server Time)
    

### Step 2 — Gather Evidence

You (or your leadership) need:

- Screenshots or video
    
- Visible **time** if possible
    
- Clear names of everyone involved
    
- 1–3 line description
    

### Step 3 — R5 / Rep Posts to NAP

R5 / designated rep will:

- post to **`#evidence`** in the NAP Discord
    
- a Clerk will log it and open a case
    

You do **not** need to personally argue in NAP Discord. That’s what your leadership + Clerks are for.

---

## 6️⃣ Appeals (If You Think It’s Wrong)

- Only **big penalties** (over 48h, bans, blacklists, major sanctions) can be appealed
    
- Appeals must go through your **R5 / alliance leadership** to `#appeals`
    
- You **keep serving the penalty** while the appeal is reviewed
    
- Council can:
    
    - reduce it (simple majority), or
        
    - overturn it (supermajority)
        

If you want to appeal, talk to your R5; don’t try to do it alone.

---

## 7️⃣ TL;DR for Regular Players

- Don’t hit bases/tiles of NAP alliances randomly
    
- Don’t plunder tasks/trucks from 1866 players
    
- Don’t drop tags to attack — ever
    
- Don’t stir cross-server drama or collude against 1866
    
- If something happens, screenshot it and tell your R5
    
- L6 is controlled by NAP rules; don’t freelance there
    
- Non-NAP alliances = unprotected, especially for land
    

If you follow **basic respect + don’t be sneaky**, you’re basically safe under NAP 9.
