# 🛡️ **NAP 9 Governance Covenant**

_Server 1866 — Central Alliance Governance Framework_

> [!summary]+ **Summary**  
> NAP 9 establishes a structured, enforceable, and limited-membership governance system for Server 1866.  
> 
> It unifies communication, penalties, evidence requirements, land governance, and decision authority under a nine-member voting Council supported by neutral Clerks.

---

# 📘 **SECTION I — Purpose, Scope & Authority**

## 🎯 **Purpose**

> [!info]  
> NAP 9 exists to:
> 
> - create a small, responsive governing body;
>     
> - standardize penalties, definitions, and evidence rules;
>     
> - enforce land governance and rotation stability;
>     
> - maintain fairness and continuity for Server 1866.
>     

## 🧭 **Scope of Application**

This Covenant applies to:

- All **Full Member (NAP 9)** alliances
    
- All **Associate Member** alliances
    
- All NAP-protected players
    
- All land-holding alliances on Server 1866
    
- All actions involving diplomacy, plundering, land control, behavior, or PvP
    

Non-members have **restricted protections**, clarified in Section II.

## 🛑 **Authority of the Council**

> [!danger]  
> The **NAP 9 Governance Council** (“the Council”) is the **sole authority** empowered to:
> 
> - interpret this Covenant
>     
> - classify offenses
>     
> - issue penalties
>     
> - manage land rotation
>     
> - regulate membership status
>     
> - issue sanctions and blacklists
>     
> - amend this Covenant via the Amendment process
>     

Council decisions are binding on **all NAP-protected alliances**.

---

# 🧱 **SECTION II — Membership Tiers**

NAP 9 recognizes **three membership states**:

1. 🔵 **Full Members** (NAP 9 voting alliances)
    
2. 🟩 **Associate Members** (protected, non-voting alliances)
    
3. 🟥 **Non-Members** (unprotected alliances)
    

---

## 🔵 **A. Full Members (NAP 9)**

Full Members are the **Top 9 Alliances** by weekly power ranking, excluding explicitly Non-Member alliances.

Each Full Member appoints:

- **1 Voting Representative** (R5 **OR** designated R4 delegate)
    
- **1 Alternate** (Designated R4 delegate)
    

Full Members have:

- full NAP protection
    
- full land protection
    
- L1–L6 land rights
    
- voting authority
    
- rotation eligibility
    

> [!tip]  
> Full Members form the nine voting seats of the Council.

---

## 🟩 **B. Associate Members (Max 7)**

Associate Members are alliances that opt into NAP protections without joining NAP 9.

> [!info]  
> Associate Members **do not** vote and **cannot** participate in L6 rotation.  
> They retain land protections for L1–L5 but **are forbidden** from holding L6 land.

### Rights:

- NAP protection
    
- Access to evidence channels
    
- May submit cases
    
- May attend meetings (non-voting)
    
- May retain L1–L5 land with protection
    

### Restrictions:

- No voting power
    
- No L6 rights
    
- No rotation privileges
    
- Cap of **7** total Associate Members
    

---

## 🟩 **Admission of Associate Members (FCFS System)**

Admission is **first-come first-serve** based on timestamped petition in:

`NAP Discord → #associate-applications`

### Eligibility:

- Accept full NAP 9 terms
    
- Maintain an active R5/Rep
    
- Not harbor blacklisted players
    
- Not be under investigation
    
- Not recently opted-out (7-day cooldown)
    

### Process:

1. **Petition submitted** in #associate-applications
    
2. **Clerk verification**
    
3. If space < 7, **automatic admission**
    
4. If full, alliance joins **waiting queue**
    

> [!success]  
> No vote is required for Associate admission unless complications arise.

---

## 🟩 **Loss, Removal, or Opt-Out**

### Opt-Out

An alliance may voluntarily opt out with a public declaration.  

**Consequences:**

- immediate loss of NAP protection
    
- immediate loss of L1–L5 protection
    
- immediate loss of all land rights
    
- **mandatory release of any L6 lands**
    
- 7-day cooldown before re-entry
    

### Removal (Simple Majority)

**Associate status may be revoked for:**

- non-responsiveness
    
- violating governance rulings
    
- harboring blacklisted players
    
- interference with evidence or cases
    

Vacant slots are filled from the waiting queue.

---

## 🟥 **C. Non-Members**

Non-Members have **no NAP protection** and no governance rights. They cannot submit evidence or create cases. Non-members are independent operators who rely on their own network of support to resolve internal and external issues within the server.

> [!warning]  
> Non-Members forfeit **all land protections**.

### Land Rights for Non-Members:

- **L6 lands:**
    
    - must be released immediately if held
        
    - if not released, NAP 9 may forcibly seize without penalty
        
- **L1–L5 lands:**
    
    - may be held
        
    - but carry **no protection**
        
    - any alliance may seize them freely
        
    - no evidence, diplomacy, or penalties apply
        

This rule is integrated into land governance (Section X).

---

# 👥 **SECTION III — Governance Council**

> [!quote]  
> The Council consists of **9 voting representatives**, one from each Full Member alliance.

Alternates only vote when the primary Rep is unavailable.

## 🎛️ **Quorum**

A quorum is the minimum required present members of a committee that must be present in order for official actions to commence. 

Quorum for any vote requires:

> **5 out of 9** votes submitted within the vote window.

Votes may be:

- ✔ YES
    
- ❌ NO
    
- ➖ ABSTAIN
    

Abstain counts toward quorum.

## ⚖️ **Voting Thresholds**

- **Simple Majority:** 5 YES
    
- **Supermajority:** 7 YES
    

### Supermajority is required for:

- Permanent blacklists
    
- Alliance expulsion
    
- Rotation removal
    
- Severe sanctions
    
- Amendments
    
- Overturning Severe Offense penalties
    

---

# 🗂️ **SECTION IV — Clerkship System (Neutral Operators)**

> [!important]  
> NAP 9 appoints **three (3)** Clerks.  
> Clerks must be from **three different NAP 9 alliances**.  
> Clerks **cannot vote** and cannot be R5s.

Clerks serve as **neutral facilitators**, not judges. The Council may function with a minimum of **one Clerk**. A lack of any appointed Clerk will suspend all Council activities until a new clerk is appointed via Emergency Vote by the Council.

---

## 📋 **Clerk Responsibilities**

- Validate and log evidence
    
- Assign Case IDs
    
- Maintain #case-log and archives
    
- Open/close vote windows
    
- Record vote results
    
- Monitor procedural compliance
    
- Issue requests for missing evidence
    
- Maintain amendment archives
    
- Notify Council of active cases
    

---

## 🧪 **Eligibility Requirements**

Clerks must:

- be members of a NAP 9 Full Member alliance
    
- not be their alliance’s R5, alternate, or designated delegate
    
- be active and responsive
    
- have no penalties in the last 30 days
    
- demonstrate neutrality and professionalism
    
- be fluent in Discord operations
    
- not share an alliance with another Clerk
    

---

## 📝 **Application & Appointment Process**

> [!done]+ **Application Steps**
> 
> 1. Announcement posted in NAP & 1866 Discord
>     
> 2. Applicant submits:
>     
>     - IGN
>         
>     - Alliance
>         
>     - Relevant experience
>         
>     - Impartiality statement
>         
> 3. R5s conduct conflict checks
>     

> [!check]+ **Appointment Vote**
> 
> - 16-hour screening vote
>     
> - 24-hour final vote
>     
> - Simple majority (≥5 YES) required
>     
> - Top candidates selected until all three seats are filled
>     

---

## ⏳ **Clerk Term, Removal, and Replacement**

### Term:

- **90 days** from conclusion of the appointment vote by the Council.
    

### Removable Offense (Simple Majority Petition):

- inactivity (3 missed cases in 14 days)
    
- misconduct
    
- evidence tampering
    
- becoming R5
    
- joining alliance of an existing Clerk
    
- failure to maintain neutrality
    

### Temporary Suspension:

A Clerk under investigation is **temporarily suspended** until cleared or removed. A suspended Clerk may not advertise themselves as able perform Clerk duties under penalty of Moderate Offense. The suspended Clerk shall be stripped of Discord role until the conclusion of the investigation.

### Replacement:

A vacancy triggers a Clerk appointment vote within 48 hours.

**NAP 9 operates across asynchronous, multi-platform communication.**  

To maintain uniformity:

> [!info] **Official Hierarchy of Platforms**  
> **1. NAP Discord** — _Authoritative_  
> **2. 1866 Public Discord** — secondary  
> **3. In-game chat/DMs/Moments** — notification only

### Only communications which partake in the official NAP Discord constitutes **official governance communication**.

---

## 🕒 **Server Time (ST) Standard**

All:

- deadlines
    
- penalties
    
- evidence
    
- vote windows
    
- timestamps
    

must use **Server Time (ST)**.

Failure to use ST may invalidate evidence or delay cases.

---

## 📢 **Announcement Protocol**

All binding decisions must be posted in:

- `#nap-announcements` (read-only)
    
- `#case-log`
    
- `#nap-council-chat` (for discussion)
    

Associate Members may view but not vote.

---

# 📎 **SECTION VI — Evidence Requirements**

> [!danger]  
> Evidence must be submitted in:  
> **NAP Discord → #evidence**

A case cannot exist until evidence is submitted here.

---

## 📑 **Required Evidence Format**

Each submission must be acceptable to support the nature of the case, and may include:

- Screenshot(s) or video
    
- Visible Server Time (when possible)
    
- Names of offender(s) and affected players
    
- ≤3 sentence description
    
- Diplomacy attempts (when required by rule)
    
- Damage summary (if applicable)
    

---

## ❌ **Invalid Evidence**

> [!failure] Evidence is invalid if:
> 
> - edited or manipulated
>     
> - cropped to hide context or timestamps
>     
> - unverifiable
>     
> - hearsay
>     
> - Discord DMs
>     
> - AI-generated images
>     
> - insufficient detail
>     

Evidence tampering triggers Severe penalties (Section VIII).

---

## ⚠️ **Evidence Correction Window**

If evidence is incomplete, Clerks may request clarification.

Submitter has **12 hours** to correct or add missing material.  
Failure → automatic invalidation.

Clerks hold discretionary power to extend this window to 24 hours under extreme circumstances.

---

## 🗃️ **Evidence Archiving**

Clerks must archive:

- all evidence media
    
- Case ID
    
- offender record
    
- classification
    
- timestamps
    
- penalty details
    
- appeals (if filed)
    

Archive must be accessible to all Full Member R5s.

---

# 🗳️ **SECTION VII — Asynchronous Voting System**

NAP 9 uses an asynchronous vote system to accommodate global time zones and multi-platform communication.

> [!note]  
> A vote is valid once **5/9** representatives cast a vote (YES / NO / ABSTAIN).

## 🕒 **Vote Windows**

> [!info]+ **🕒 Long Votes — 24 Hours**  
> Required for high-impact decisions:
> 
> - Amendments
>     
> - Appeals
>     
> - Permanent blacklists
>     
> - Alliance expulsions
>     
> - Rotation changes
>     
> - Structural governance decisions
>     

> [!info]+ **🕓 Standard Votes — 16 Hours**  
> Required for:
> 
> - Moderate penalties
>     
> - Diplomacy rulings
>     
> - Routine governance matters
>     
> - Non-emergency sanctions
>     

> [!info]+ **🕗 Emergency Votes — 8 Hours**  
> Required for:
> 
> - Active land conflicts
>     
> - Cross-server attacks
>     
> - Immediate ceasefires
>     
> - Active tag-drop incidents
>     
> - Seizure of L6 lands from Non-Members
>     

---

## 🧮 **Vote Participation Requirements**

An alliance is considered _non-participatory_ if, in a 14 day period:

- it misses **2 emergency votes**, OR
    
- it misses **3 votes of any type**.
    

Consequences (Simple Majority):

- suspension from rotation
    
- downgrade to Associate Member
    
- temporary revocation of voting rights
    

Non-participation resets after 14 days.

---

## ⚖️ **Vote Result Validity**

A vote is valid if:

- quorum (5/9) is reached
    
- the vote window expires, OR
    
- all 9 votes are submitted early
    

### **YES Thresholds**

- **5 YES** → Simple Majority
    
- **7 YES** → Supermajority
    

### **Abstain**

- counts toward quorum
    
- not a YES
    
- not a NO
    

### **Absent**

- does _not_ count toward quorum
    
- may trigger non-participation penalties
    

---

# ⚖️ **SECTION VIII — Offense Categories & Penalties**

This is the judicial core of the Covenant.  
All offenses fall into **Minor**, **Moderate**, or **Severe** categories.

Escalation rules apply across categories.

---

# 🟨 **MINOR OFFENSES**

> [!summary]  
> Minor = accidental, low-impact, low-intent violations.

## 📝 **Examples**

- accidental tile hits
    
- accidental troop contact
    
- mild diplomacy delays
    
- minor chat misconduct
    
- minor disruption of L6 handoff (non-malicious)
    

## 📄 **Required Evidence**

- timestamped screenshot/video
    
- ≤3 sentence description
    
- summary of damage (if any)
    
- diplomacy context (when relevant)
    

## 🟩 **Standard Penalties**

- logged formal warning
    
- restitution for losses
    
- **24–48 hour Probation**
    
    - no non-capitol PvP
        
    - no aggressive tile movement
        
    - probation violation → auto-escalates to Moderate
        

## ⚖️ **Mitigating Factors**

- immediate apology
    
- self-reporting
    
- clean 30-day history
    

## 🔺 **Aggravating Factors**

- repeated minor violations
    
- non-responsiveness
    
- neglect of diplomacy
    

---

# 🟧 **MODERATE OFFENSES**

> [!warning]  
> Meaningful harm, repeated violations, or moderate intent.

## 📝 **Examples**

- repeated truck/task plundering
    
- aggressive tile hits without tag drop
    
- pattern of diplomacy refusal
    
- obstructing L6 temporary drops
    
- violating Minor Offense probation
    

## 📄 **Required Evidence**

- timestamps
    
- pattern of behavior
    
- diplomacy attempts (required)
    
- damage/loss summary
    
- Clerk case validation
    

## 🟧 **Standard Penalties**

- mandatory restitution
    
- **48–72 hour Combat Restriction**
    
    - no offensive PvP outside Capitol
        
- **Temporary Rotation Suspension**
    
- placement on **Moderate Watchlist** (14 days)
    

## ⚖️ **Mitigating Factors**

- rapid restitution
    
- full cooperation
    

## 🔺 **Aggravating Factors**

- repetition within 30 days
    
- multi-player involvement
    
- disregard of council instructions
    

---

# 🟥 **SEVERE OFFENSES**

> [!danger]  
> High intent, high impact, or destabilizing actions.

## 📝 **Examples**

- coordinated attacks on NAP alliances
    
- L6 land theft
    
- cross-server coordination against 1866
    
- targeted harassment
    
- penalty evasion (ban dodging, tag hopping)
    
- evidence tampering
    
- obstructing Council votes
    
- repeated Moderate offenses
    

## 📄 **Required Evidence**

- verified timestamps
    
- multi-source evidence where possible
    
- diplomacy logs
    
- impact statement
    
- Clerk validation
    

---

## ⚖️ **Penalties — Simple Majority (≥5 YES)**

These are Severe but not existential threats:

- **72-hour serverwide ban** (offender must remain untagged)
    
- **7-day ban from all alliances**
    
- restitution
    
- Severe Offense record logged
    

---

## ⚖️ **Penalties — Supermajority (≥7 YES)**

> [!danger]  
> Reserved for malicious, repeated, or cross-server threats.

- **Permanent Blacklist**
    
- **Alliance Sanctions**, including:
    
    - rotation removal
        
    - downgrading to Associate Member
        
    - temporary expulsion from NAP
        
- **Permanent Rotation Removal**
    
- **Permanent Alliance Expulsion**
    
- **Serverwide KOS designation**
    

---

|Trigger|Escalation|Notes|
|---|---|---|

# 🛡️ **SECTION IX — Tag-Dropping Blacklist System**

This is the only **automatic** escalation rule in NAP 9.

> [!danger]  
> Tag-drop aggression has its own unique penalty structure.

## 🟪 **Instance One**

- **48-hour ban** from all alliances
    
- offender must remain **untagged**
    
- any alliance sheltering the offender loses NAP protection
    

## 🟥 **Instance Two**

> [!failure]  
> **Automatic Permanent Blacklist**  
> Overturn requires supermajority.

---

# 🏴‍☠️ **Harboring Rule (All Blacklists)**

If an alliance shelters:

- a banned player
    
- a blacklisted player
    
- someone evading penalties
    

Then:

- the alliance **loses all NAP protection immediately**
    
- voting privileges are suspended
    
- L6 and rotation eligibility revoked
    

Protection is restored only after:

- the player leaves the alliance **and**
    
- the Council confirms compliance

# 🗺️ **SECTION X — Land Governance**


# 🟦 **1. General Land Principles**

> [!info]  
> Land is a regulated server resource.  
> All alliances must follow diplomatic expectations and rotation structure.

- Lands are considered **settled** holdings.
    
- No surprise takeovers; diplomacy is required.
    
- Aggressive land theft is a **Severe Offense**.
    

---

# 🟧 **2. Level 6 (L6) Land Rules**

There are **three (3)** L6 lands on the map.  
These lands follow a strict rotation pattern.

## 🔄 **Weekly Rotation**

- L6 rotates weekly among **Top 3 Alliances** by Power Ranking.
    
- Based on snapshot provided by (1) clerk, or in absence, (2) any NAP 9 Leader or Delegate, **one hour before VS Day 6 reset**
    
- Rotation occurs **Sunday @ 12:00 ST**
    
- Rotation is **clockwise**
    
- Temporary drops must be honored
    
- Alliances must pick up land within **4 hours**
    

> [!warning]  
> Holding a temporary drop is a **Moderate Offense** and may escalate.

---

# 🟥 **3. Non-Member Land Restrictions**

> [!danger]  
> Non-Members forfeit **all land protection** and **all L6 rights**.

### Upon transitioning from Full-Member to Non-Member:

**Immediate Consequences:**

- **Immediate release of all Level 6 lands**
    
    - If not cooperatively scheduled for release, Council may authorize a coordinated **forced seizure** with compulsory participation by all NAP 9 beneficiaries.
        
- **Zero protection** for L1–L5 lands
    
    - Any alliance may seize L1–L5 lands freely
        
    - No evidence rules apply
        
    - No penalties exist for attacks on their lands
        

### Non-Members Cannot:

- hold L6 lands
    
- claim L6 rotation rights
    
- invoke NAP protection
    
- submit evidence
    

This ensures land stability while preventing exploitation.

---

# 🟩 **4. Associate Member Land Rules**

Associate Members:

- may hold L1–L5 lands with full protection
    
- may not hold L6 lands
    
- if applicable, must release held L6 lands upon becoming an Associate Member
    
- may claim restitution for L1–L5 violations
    
- may not join L6 rotation
    

---

# 🔵 **5. Full Member Land Rules**

Full Members:

- fully protected for L1–L5
    
- eligible for L6 rotation
    
- must honor temporary drops
    
- must coordinate land transfers
    

L6 violations escalate to **Moderate or Severe** depending on intent.

---

# ⚔️ **6. Capitol Zone Combat Rules**


> [!info]  
> The Capitol Mud + Capitol resource tiles are designated **Free Combat Zones**, but _only with mutual consent_.

Rules:

- combat allowed only after both sides agree
    
- gathering tile hits without consent require **equal-power restitution hits** (“eye for an eye”)
    
- Capitol combat cannot justify retaliation outside the Capitol
    

Capitol combat evidence uses normal rules.

---

# 🗃️ **SECTION XI — Case Processing**

This section defines the required workflow for every case.  
The Council cannot act until all mandatory steps are completed.

---

# 🟦 **1. Case Workflow Overview**

> [!note]+ **Three Mandatory Steps**  
> **1. Notification** — Any platform  
> **2. Evidence Submission** — `#evidence`  
> **3. Clerk Log Entry** — `#case-log`

Council deliberation may **only** begin after Step 3.

---

# 🟦 **2. Step One — Notification**

Anyone may initiate a case by notifying any R5 or Clerk via:

- World chat
    
- Alliance chat
    
- In-game DM
    
- Discord DM
    
- Discord server
    
- Moments feed
    

Notification is **not** evidence.

> [!warning]  
> Notification only signals an event occurred.  
> It does _not_ create a case.

Acceptable notification content:

- “X attacked Y, evidence incoming.”
    
- time/location
    
- involved parties
    

Unacceptable:

- calls for punishment
    
- cropped or edited images
    
- argument escalations
    

---

# 🟦 **3. Step Two — Evidence Submission**

Evidence must be posted to:

> **NAP Discord → `#evidence`**

Required contents:

- screenshot(s) or video
    
- visible Server Time (if possible)
    
- names of offender(s)
    
- names of affected party
    
- ≤3 sentence description
    
- diplomacy attempts (if rule requires it)
    
- loss/damage summary
    

### **Correction Window**

If incomplete, Clerks may request clarification.  
Submitter has **6 hours** to correct; failure invalidates case.

---

# 🟦 **4. Step Three — Clerk Log Entry**

A Clerk must:

1. Assign a **Case ID**
    
2. Post it in `#case-log` including:
    
    - offender(s)
        
    - reporting alliance
        
    - evidence summary
        
    - preliminary offense category
        
    - timestamp
        
3. Mark the case as **ACTIVE**
    
4. Notify Council for activation
    

> [!danger]  
> Until this happens, the Council is **barred** from:
> 
> - voting
>     
> - classifying
>     
> - issuing penalties
>     
> - escalating
>     
> - debating consequences
>     

If no Clerk responds within 2 hours:

- R5s may ping Clerks directly
    
- If 4 hours pass, a temporary Clerk may be appointed via emergency vote (8hr window)
    

---

# 🟦 **5. Council Activation**

Once a case is ACTIVE:

- discussion may begin
    
- investigation can proceed
    
- Clerks may open vote windows
    
- alliances may issue statements
    
- classification can occur
    

Deliberation before Step 3 is void.

---

# 🟦 **6. Case Classification**

Cases are classified as:

- **Minor Offense**
    
- **Moderate Offense**
    
- **Severe Offense**
    
- **Exceptional Case** (needs amendment-level ruling)
    

Classification determines the vote window and penalty tier.

Clerks update Case ID with classification tag.

---

# 🟦 **7. Consolidated Cases**

Clerks may consolidate incidents if:

- same offender
    
- same event window
    
- same evidence bundle
    
- same victim alliance
    
- consolidation improves clarity
    

Consolidation must not reduce procedural rights.

---

# 🟦 **8. Duplicate or Frivolous Cases**

Clerks may mark cases as:

### **Duplicate**

- references an existing case
    
- no new evidence provided
    

### **Frivolous**

- lacks substance
    
- submitted in bad faith
    
- retaliatory
    
- violates conduct rules
    

Frivolous submissions may escalate to **Minor or Moderate Offense**.

---

# 🟦 **9. Case Closure**

A case closes when:

- penalties issued
    
- case dismissed
    
- appeal window expires
    
- appeal processed
    
- offender leaves server
    
- offender deleted by devs (rare)
    

Clerks must:

- mark case as **Resolved**, **Dismissed**, or **Archived**
    
- record penalty timestamps
    
- store evidence in archives
    
- update offender’s history for escalation tracking
    

---

# 🟦 **10. Case Workflow Summary Table**

|Step|Name|Action|Responsible|
|---|---|---|---|
|1|Notification|Initial alert|Anyone|
|2|Evidence|Submission to #evidence|Reporting party|
|3|Clerk Log Entry|Assign Case ID|Clerk|
|4|Council Activation|Discussion + investigation|Council|
|5|Classification|Minor / Moderate / Severe|Council|
|6|Resolution|Penalty / dismissal|Council|
|7|Archival|Case moved to archive|Clerk|

# 🔁 **SECTION XII — Appeals**

Appeals allow players and alliances to challenge penalties under structured, time-bound rules.

---

# 🟦 **1. Appeal Eligibility**

> [!info] Only penalties **over 48 hours** may be appealed.

Eligible:

- 72-hour bans
    
- 7-day bans
    
- rotation suspensions
    
- loss of NAP protection
    
- alliance-level sanctions
    
- Severe Offense penalties
    
- permanent blacklists → once only
    

Not eligible:

- warnings
    
- 24–48 hour probations
    
- restitution-only penalties
    
- Minor Offense rulings
    
- first tag-drop (48 hours)
    

---

# 🟦 **2. Filing an Appeal**

Must be submitted within:

> **48 hours of penalty issuance**  
> to `#appeals` channel.

Required components:

- Case ID
    
- offender’s IGN
    
- alliance
    
- penalty being appealed
    
- grounds for appeal:
    
    - misclassification
        
    - procedural error
        
    - misinterpretation
        
    - new evidence
        
    - disproportionate penalty
        
- supporting evidence
    

Clerks reject incomplete or ineligible appeals.

---

# 🟦 **3. Clerk Intake & Validation**

Clerk must:

1. validate eligibility
    
2. request corrections (if needed)
    
3. assign an **Appeal ID**
    
4. log it into `#case-log`
    
5. notify Council
    

If corrections are required, appellant has **6 hours** to comply.

---

# 🟦 **4. Investigative Review**

Review is handled by:

> The Clerk assigned to the case or any delegated Clerk.

Time limit:

> **24 hours**

Review includes:

- evidence re-check
    
- procedural analysis
    
- timestamp verification
    
- cross-referencing submissions
    
- examining new evidence
    
- contacting parties if needed
    

Clerk then posts a summary with a recommended outcome.

---

# 🟦 **5. Council Vote on Appeal**

After summary posting:

> **24-hour Long Vote** begins.

Vote options:

- **YES** → grant appeal
    
- **NO** → deny
    
- **ABSTAIN** → neutral (counts toward quorum)
    

Quorum: 5 votes.

---

# 🟦 **6. Appeal Outcomes**

> [!info] Thresholds:

- **Simple Majority (≥5 YES)** → reduce penalty
    
- **Supermajority (≥7 YES)** → overturn penalty entirely
    

Failure to meet either = appeal denied.

### Reduction may include:

- shorter ban
    
- lowered classification
    
- removal of rotation suspension
    

### Overturn results in:

- reversal of penalty
    
- correction notice in `#nap-announcements`
    
- cleaning of offender’s record
    

---

# 🟦 **7. Restrictions During Appeal**

Offender must continue serving penalty.  
Alliances must enforce it.

> [!danger]  
> Violating penalty during appeal = **automatic appeal denial + Severe escalation.**

---

# 🟦 **8. Finalization & Archival**

Clerks must:

- update case file
    
- record vote totals
    
- adjust timestamps
    
- archive appeal
    
- publish summary in announcements
    

---

# 🟦 **9. Appeal Limits**

- 1 appeal per 30 days (per player or alliance)
    
- permanent blacklist → one appeal only
    
- cannot challenge:
    
    - evidence validity
        
    - voting rules
        
    - amendments
        
    - previously denied appeals

# 🚪 **SECTION XIII — Opt-Outs & Expulsions**

This section defines how alliances leave or are removed from NAP 9.

---

## 🟥 **A. Opt-Out**

Alliance may opt out with a declaration in:

> `#nap-announcements`

Immediate consequences:

- lose NAP protection
    
- forfeit all L1–L5 protection
    
- must release all L6 lands immediately
    
- may be forcibly seized if not released
    
- 7-day cooldown before re-entry
    

---

## 🟥 **B. Expulsion (Supermajority)**

Grounds include:

- harboring blacklisted players
    
- refusing to enforce penalties
    
- repeated non-responsiveness
    
- undermining Council operations
    
- coordinated misconduct
    
- cross-server collusion
    

Consequences mirror Opt-Out + loss of rights for 30 days.

---

## 🟨 **C. Reinstatement**

Reinstatement requires:

- filing a petition
    
- simple majority approval
    
- immediate compliance with all outstanding rulings

# 📝 **SECTION XIV — Amendments**

This section governs revisions to the Covenant.

> [!summary]  
> Amendments require a **Supermajority (7/9 YES)** and follow a multi-step procedure.

---

# 🟦 **1. Who May Propose Amendments**

- any Full Member R5 or delegate
    
- any Clerk (procedural amendments only)
    
- **three Associate Members acting jointly**
    

Players may petition their R5, but cannot propose directly.

---

# 🟦 **2. Amendment Requirements**

A valid amendment must specify:

- section/subsection to modify
    
- full text of revision
    
- justification (1–5 sentences)
    
- classification:
    
    - additive
        
    - revisory
        
    - repeal
        
    - structural
        
- retroactivity or not
    
- activation timing
    
- sunset clause (optional)
    

Clerks reject incomplete proposals.

---

# 🟦 **3. Notice & Posting**

Clerks must post proposals to:

- `#nap-proposals`
    
- `#nap-announcements`
    

A **24-hour Notice Period** is mandatory.

---

# 🟦 **4. Discussion Period**

Optional but encouraged.  
If substantive edits occur:

> The 24-hour Notice Period resets.

---

# 🟦 **5. Voting Procedure**

After Notice Period:

- 24-hour Long Vote
    
- quorum of 5
    
- **7 YES** required for adoption
    

Abstentions count toward quorum; absences do not.

---

# 🟦 **6. Activation**

If passed:

- amendment takes immediate effect unless scheduled
    
- contradicting text is superseded
    
- Clerks mark outdated language as **RETIRED**
    

---

# 🟦 **7. If an Amendment Fails**

- may not be reintroduced for 7 days
    
- if two Clerks concur, a **substantially revised** version may be submitted immediately
    

---

# 🟦 **8. Emergency Amendments**

Allowed only when:

- ≥5 R5s invoke emergency pathway
    
- server integrity at risk
    

Process:

1. 1-hour Notice
    
2. 8-hour Vote
    
3. requires 7 YES
    

Emergency amendments expire after **30 days** unless formally adopted.

---

# 🟦 **9. Conflict of Interest**

R5 must recuse when:

- the amendment targets their alliance
    
- their position
    
- grants their alliance unique privileges
    

Alternate votes instead.  
If both are conflicted, the seat auto-abstains.

---

# 🟦 **10. Clerk Archive Requirements**

Clerks must store:

- Amendment ID
    
- before/after text
    
- vote breakdown
    
- activation/sunset info
    
- conflict-of-interest substitutions
    
- discussion records
    

Archive must be accessible to all R5s.

---

# 🟦 **11. Amendment Formatting Standards**

All amendments must:

- follow NAP 9 heading structure
    
- use clear, deterministic language (MUST / SHALL / MUST NOT)
    
- avoid vague suggestionss
    

---
