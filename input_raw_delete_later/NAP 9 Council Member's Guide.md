# 📘 **NAP 9 Council Member Guide**

_Operational Manual for R5s & Delegates of the NAP 9 Governance Council_  
_Server 1866_

---

# 🏛️ SECTION 0 — Purpose of the Council

The Council is the **nine-alliance governing body** of Server 1866.  
Its function is to:

- maintain server stability
    
- adjudicate disputes
    
- classify offenses
    
- vote on penalties
    
- govern land rotation
    
- enforce the NAP Covenant
    
- amend the Covenant when needed
    

This guide defines your responsibilities, limits, and procedures.

---

# 🧭 SECTION 1 — Council Structure & Role Definitions

## 1.1 Council Composition

The Council consists of:

- **9 Voting Representatives** (one per NAP 9 Full Member alliance)
    
- **Alternates** (one per Full Member alliance; vote only when primary is absent)
    
- **3 Clerks** (non-voting, procedural operators)
    

## 1.2 Your Role as a Council Member

You are responsible for:

- representing your alliance’s interests
    
- participating in **all votes**
    
- reviewing evidence
    
- discussing cases professionally
    
- engaging in diplomacy
    
- maintaining compliance with the Covenant
    
- preventing your alliance from violating rules
    
- monitoring your members’ behavior
    

## 1.3 You Are _Not_ Responsible For:

- judging intent (focus on behavior, outcomes, and evidence)
    
- arguing penalty severity outside Council rules
    
- moderating discord conflicts
    
- interpreting or modifying procedure (Clerks handle this)
    
- handling evidence (only Clerks validate)
    
- acting unilaterally without Council approval
    

Your authority exists **only** within the vote system.

---

# 🎓 SECTION 2 — Professional Standards for R5s & Delegates

Council members must:

- communicate clearly and briefly
    
- avoid inflammatory tone
    
- never threaten other alliances
    
- follow Server Time
    
- maintain confidentiality of Council discussions
    
- keep records for their alliance
    
- treat Clerks respectfully
    
- avoid disruptive behavior
    

**Toxicity, bullying, or aggressive rhetoric undermine vote legitimacy** and can be penalized.

---

# 🧱 SECTION 3 — Participation Requirements

Council Members must:

- vote in **≥80% of votes**
    
- respond to evidence requests quickly
    
- monitor the Council channels
    
- maintain easy contact with Clerks
    
- remain reachable during active conflicts
    

### Missing Votes → Penalties

If your alliance misses:

- **2 Emergency Votes** _or_
    
- **3 Votes Total in 14 Days
    

The Council may:

- suspend your rotation rights
    
- downgrade you to Associate Member
    
- revoke voting rights temporarily
    

Consistency matters.

---

# 📢 SECTION 4 — Communication Protocols

## 4.1 Official Channels

Council members must use:

- **NAP Discord → #nap-council-chat** (deliberation)
    
- **NAP Discord → #case-log** (ongoing cases)
    
- **NAP Discord → #nap-announcements** (official rulings)
    
- **NAP Discord → #nap-proposals** (amendments)
    

**In-game chat is never binding** for governance decisions.

## 4.2 Communication Rules

- No case may proceed without a Clerk marking it ACTIVE.
    
- No vote may begin until Clerks open the window.
    
- Do not DM Clerks evidence.
    
- Do not demand Clerks rush or delay cases.
    

---

# 🗂️ SECTION 5 — Case Participation

## 5.1 Your Responsibilities in Cases

When a case becomes ACTIVE:

You must:

- read the Case ID post
    
- review the evidence
    
- compare behavior to offense categories
    
- ask clarifying questions if needed
    
- participate in discussion
    
- cast your vote
    

## 5.2 How to Challenge Evidence (Properly)

If something appears off:

You may request that a Clerk:

- re-check timestamps
    
- verify alliance membership
    
- validate sequence of events
    
- confirm evidence completeness
    

You may NOT:

- accuse the submitter of lying
    
- attack the Clerk
    
- request penalties before vote window opens
    
- argue from screenshots posted outside #evidence
    

## 5.3 How to Classify Offenses

Council members classify cases based on:

- intent (if clear)
    
- repetition
    
- damage
    
- diplomacy failures
    
- rule text
    

Reference Section VIII of the Covenant.

# 🗳️ SECTION 6 — Voting Responsibilities

Voting is your most important duty.

## 6.1 When to Vote

Vote **every time a Clerk opens a window**.

You must:

- read the vote prompt
    
- check the vote window duration (8/16/24 hours)
    
- evaluate evidence
    
- coordinate with your alliance
    
- submit YES / NO / ABSTAIN
    

## 6.2 How to Vote

Post clearly:

> **GMOB — YES**  
> **GMOB — NO**  
> **GMOB — ABSTAIN**

Do **not** leave ambiguous votes such as:

- “probably yes”
    
- “we lean no”
    
- reactions/emojis
    

These do not count.

## 6.3 What ABSTAIN Means

ABSTAIN:

- counts toward quorum
    
- is neutral
    
- does not support either outcome
    
- is appropriate when unbiased judgment is impossible
    

## 6.4 You Cannot Change Your Vote

Once cast, votes are:

- **locked**
    
- **non-editable**
    
- **non-retractable**
    

Clerks enforce this strictly.

## 6.5 Quorum Rules

Quorum = **5 votes**  
Once reached:

- discussion can still continue
    
- vote remains open until window ends
    

## 6.6 Early Completion

If all 9 votes submitted early:

- Clerk may close early
    
- outcome posts immediately

# 🧭 SECTION 7 — Conflict of Interest Rules

You MUST recuse (or Abstain) when:

- your alliance is the offender
    
- your alliance is the victim
    
- amendment targets your alliance
    
- your rotation or land rights are directly involved
    
- Clerk investigation targets someone in your alliance
    

If conflict exists:

- your Alternate votes instead
    
- if both conflicted → your seat auto-Abstains
    

Do NOT attempt to influence others on a conflicted vote.

---

# 📜 SECTION 8 — How to Initiate Council Actions

## 8.1 How to Initiate a Case

You cannot open cases directly.  
Players must submit evidence to `#evidence`.

However, you **can** alert Clerks:

> “Evidence pending; expect submission shortly.”

## 8.2 How to Request a Vote

You may request:

- Minor → Moderate → Severe escalation review
    
- Emergency vote
    
- Land ruling
    
- Penalty clarification
    

Clerk will schedule it _if appropriate_.

## 8.3 How to Request an Appeal

Your alliance can:

- file an appeal
    
- assist a member in filing
    
- submit new evidence
    

Appeals must follow Section XII rules.

# 🌍 SECTION 9 — Land Governance Duties

Council members must:

### 9.1 Maintain L6 Rotation Integrity

You are responsible for:

- ensuring your alliance drops L6 when required
    
- protecting your alliance’s rotation rights
    
- reporting rotation violations
    
- coordinating temporary drops
    

### 9.2 Enforce Non-Member Land Rules

Non-Members:

- must release L6 immediately
    
- have no land protections
    
- may be freely displaced
    

Council members should:

- ensure seizures are coordinated
    
- avoid unnecessary escalation
    

### 9.3 Resolve Land Conflicts

Land conflicts are typically:

- Moderate if accidental
    
- Severe if malicious
    
- Emergency if active battle ongoing

# 🛡 SECTION 10 — Handling Disputes & Diplomacy

R5s are expected to:

- de-escalate where possible
    
- avoid threats
    
- avoid inflammatory language
    
- provide complete diplomacy logs when required
    
- never attempt to circumvent evidence procedure
    

If diplomacy fails → submit evidence.

If conduct turns hostile → Clerk may classify as separate offense.

---

# 🚨 SECTION 11 — Emergency Responsibilities

Emergencies include:

- active attacks
    
- tag-drop aggression
    
- L6 conflicts
    
- cross-server threats
    

Council members must:

1. alert Clerks
    
2. request Emergency Vote
    
3. provide timestamped evidence
    
4. coordinate ceasefire or retaliation as permitted
    

---

# 📁 SECTION 12 — Working with Clerks

## 12.1 How to Interact with Clerks

- be concise
    
- ask direct procedural questions
    
- avoid repeating requests
    
- do not pressure Clerks about outcomes
    
- log disagreements professionally
    

Clerks control **procedure**,  
Council controls **outcome**.

## 12.2 When Clerks Are Wrong (Rare)

If a Clerk makes a procedural error:

- notify them publicly in `#nap-council-chat`
    
- cite the rule
    
- avoid personalizing
    
- request correction
    

If ignored:

- escalate to the other 2 Clerks
    
- request a Council Review
    

---

# 📚 SECTION 13 — Best Practices for Effective Council Members

- Vote early, vote consistently
    
- Maintain your own logs
    
- Give feedback privately to your alliance
    
- Avoid long arguments
    
- Ask Clerks for clarification, not interpretation
    
- Avoid “gut votes”—use rules
    
- Avoid assuming intent
    
- Stay calm during emergencies
    
- Keep discussions fact-focused
    

---

# 🧨 SECTION 14 — Council Member Red Flags

You may be violating your duties if:

- you’re voting based on alliance politics
    
- you’re influencing other reps off-record
    
- you’re missing votes
    
- you’re disrespecting Clerks
    
- your alliance repeatedly violates rules
    
- you delay compliance
    
- you argue excessively
    
- you demand special treatment
    
- you lobby for outcomes during appeals
    

Repeated red flags may justify:

- probation
    
- loss of rotation rights
    
- downgrade to Associate Member
    
- expulsion
